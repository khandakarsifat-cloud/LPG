## Data and transaction model

- Saved sale details and bounded inventory history use `get_sale_details` / `get_inventory_history` to read their source and private actor attribution in one statement snapshot. These RPCs enforce trusted tenancy and the same source-read capabilities; the actor registry remains unexposed. Customer money/bottle dues filters are inclusive, with Both as the intersection and None as the complement.
- Most business identities use `(tenant_id, entity_id)` keys and tenant-qualified relationships. A UUID alone is not a global ownership guarantee. Auth profiles connect real Auth users to one business; RBAC assignments/mappings determine access. Browser capability checks provide navigation, while database RLS, grants and RPC gates enforce authorization independently. See the [authorization guide](../supabase/authorization.md) rather than deriving access from role names or user metadata.
- Direct browser writes are restricted to zero-stock item registration, customer creation and authorized tenant printer upserts. Business mutations derive actor/tenant server-side; purchase RPCs insert referenced stock movements under purchase permission without granting general stock adjustment.
- `items` stores current filled/empty balances, with a business category: LPG requires relational brand/weight identity and stove products require a name and configurable type; names and weight have no duplicate legacy columns. One shared polled inventory query supplies catalog forms, POS and balances. Inserting `inventory_movements` applies deltas through a trigger. Sales couple header/lines, stock, customer transactions, invoice charges, attributed payments and wallet entries. A single ledger trigger applies all wallet balances, including purchase/logistics effects. Tenant-scoped checkout keys serialize matching retries and retain the original response; changed payloads are rejected. The browser retains uncertain POS/purchase-creation requests per tenant/user for recovery. SQL owns active-price and finite-money validation. Each sale captures its own retail/wholesale `sale_tier`; selecting a saved customer reprices the entire cart and locks pricing to that customer type, enforced again by checkout SQL. Draft tier choice is available before customer selection. The sale-tier migration must precede its dependent frontend. Purchases couple stock dispatch/receipt, transits and wallet effects. Dispatch transport is internal transit revenue and never moves wallet money; normal payments and source-backed refund obligations use immutable `purchase_payments`, the existing ledger trigger and Finance request recovery. Received purchases never cancel; OWNER confirmation stops operations before separate refund settlement. Purchase refunds and final transit completion remain independent. Vehicle assignments retain history; transits snapshot crew, expose deferred earnings, and use the existing employee payment/ledger architecture. Gas plants live in Settings. [Sales](modules/sales-and-inventory.md) and [purchases](modules/purchases-and-logistics.md) explain the different timing and accounting coverage.
- Original immutable sale lines define bottle expectations; immutable receipt lines record actual received products. All sales share this model after one-time development normalization. There is no obligation mirror, tracking flag or allocation model. Receipts remain dynamic: `get_sale_receipt` returns original sale quantities/prices/discounts, current tenant/customer/product identity, all bottle receipts and current financial settlement in one statement snapshot. Preview and print use the same loader and `buildSaleReceipt`; no receipt snapshot is stored. Settlement costs reuse receipt `fee`, sale `additional_charges` and the customer ledger. They create no wallet entry until a normal payment is received. Historical expense evidence remains immutable.
- Original sale lines, employee terms, salary months/payments, purchase activity, sale payments and wallet/customer ledger records have UPDATE/DELETE rejection triggers. Inventory history has restricted browser writes. Do not reconstruct balances from transaction sums without checking coverage and historical balances. Audit triggers cover brands and vehicles; purchases also keep an attributed lifecycle/financial history.
- [Migrations](../supabase/migrations/) are an ordered chain: baseline domain DDL, fixes/customer rules, employee and printer additions, reconciliation, then authorization and printer-upsert grants. Later definitions supersede earlier functions/policies. Application SQL depends on Supabase roles, Auth schemas and extensions; it does not install a complete platform. Compose startup/build does not apply arbitrary application migrations to existing data.

## Workspace lifecycle and cache behavior

1. Auth restores/listens for the session. The provider reads the caller's `user_profiles`/tenant identity and calls `get_my_permissions`; profile and permissions refresh every 30 seconds and on query focus.
2. Registration signs up and signs in first, then calls `setup_business`. That RPC locks the Auth identity and atomically creates the tenant, profile, roles, mappings and owner assignment; a second membership is rejected. Signup itself is outside this transaction, so setup failure signs out but does not delete the Auth account.
3. Hooks perform RLS-scoped reads or RPC mutations and invalidate affected query families. Most domain keys are not tenant-keyed; the provider clears the cache when Auth user identity changes and on sign-out. Preserve that behavior when altering workspace admission/caching.
4. The query client defaults to five-minute freshness and one read retry. Dashboard, inventory and purchases add polling. Mutation invalidations must follow cross-module effects, not just the edited screen. There is no offline synchronization layer.
5. [The web manifest](../public/manifest.webmanifest), linked from `index.html`, declares standalone display and stable root identity/start URL/scope. Hash navigation and password sign-in remain within that scope. Vite copies the manifest/icons into `dist`; [the deployment check](../deploy/pwa.mjs) rejects missing assets or changed identity/scope/display before Wrangler publication. There is no service worker or application-shell cache; new launches/reloads use normal network revalidation. Legacy browser shortcuts may require reinstallation as described in [the README](../README.md#installed-android-app).

Dashboard statistics and its eight-sale page use the same Asia/Dhaka day boundary, independent of the browser's timezone. Later customer settlement costs contribute revenue on their receipt date through the existing exchange-charge mechanism. Historical business collection expenses retain their original wallet accounting. Customer count is all tenant customers, and movement count counts rows, not cylinders. The sales feed reads eight completed sales per page plus a look-ahead row since that boundary, and falls back to header-only reads if embeds fail.

## Implemented scope and limits

Operational screens for sales, stock, customers, purchases, vehicles/transits, brands/plants and employees/payments are implemented. Preserve these narrower boundaries:

- Finance provides wallets, ledger filtering and customer dues/collections. Dashboard stock exceptions remain a placeholder.
- Inventory price/commission writes require inventory management capability and trusted OWNER membership. `get_product_inventory` returns the UI's edit-authority flag, current refill/package commission references, selling prices, recorded carrying-value pools and eight weekly aggregates. Commission history is append-only and has no browser read grants. Settings persists brand order; inventory groups by that order, then ascending product weight. Inventory history filters movements before pagination, reuses stock tiles for multi-selection, and opens existing sale/purchase dialogs under their original read permissions. Employee profiles are not Auth memberships.
- Owner-only valued inventory adjustments reconcile stock, carrying-value pools and balanced journals atomically. Purchase cancellation reverses recorded stock and payments atomically; purchase receipt and custom Return vehicle release the vehicle; final transit completion requires fully paid crew earnings and settles only non-employee costs.
- [attendance-receiver](../attendance-receiver/) is the canonical internal ZKTeco ADMS service; the standalone listener prototype has been removed.
- Receipts support the browser print dialog and direct Windows USB/Ethernet through the existing bridge, packaged with its runtime as a standalone executable. A native silent Windows service starts before login, depends on Spooler, contains its child processes and uses Windows crash recovery. The installer migrates legacy per-user job history to protected ProgramData and preserves settings/journals across updates; the manual launcher starts/restarts the service. USB needs machine-wide queues/fonts visible to SYSTEM. Output preference stays in this browser; printer configuration stays tenant-wide in Supabase. Receipt persistence, API acceptance and physical output are distinct boundaries; see the [printing guide](../print-bridge/README.md).

## Accounting and analytics boundaries

The [accounting guide](modules/financial-analysis.md) is the current map of recognition, opening positions, inventory costing and reconciliation. `accounting_journals` / `accounting_lines` are the single financial source of truth. Source triggers recognize sales, purchases, monthly salary accruals, bonuses and operating costs. A locked private source projection appends corrections; wallet ledger entries settle those same control accounts. `inventory_costs` and immutable `inventory_cost_entries` keep FIFO values consistent with the physical stock ledger, including dispatched empties and customer bottle assets.

`useAccounting` owns report, historical breakdown, reconciliation-health, inventory-adjustment and Finance-management contracts. `Finance/Analysis` presents one paired opening/closing Overview, accumulated income, three charts and server-paged drill-downs. Private monthly savepoints accelerate cumulative positions; source writes atomically invalidate affected cutoffs. Customer lifetime projections update alongside their canonical sources; existing stock/wallet/loan/asset balances are reused. Private pg_cron jobs drain changed-record reconciliation at 03:00 Asia/Dhaka and resume weekly/month-end sweeps. [Accounting](modules/financial-analysis.md) defines cutoff, lock, repair and audit invariants. `AccountingManagement` reuses recoverable Finance requests for loans, assets, opening balances and incurred operating obligations. `finance_obligations` is the shared settlement projection for supplemental opening entries, unpaid business costs and credit-funded assets. It never substitutes for sale, purchase, payroll or loan obligations. All accounting mutation tables are RPC-only with RLS and explicit owner gates where required.

`useVehicleAccounting` retains grouped service/operating costs and their independent cash settlement. Asset book values and disposal are journaled through `business_assets`; vehicle `availability` is independent of journey location/status. New transits reject out-of-service/retired vehicles in SQL. Retirement preserves historical journeys/maintenance/crew and ends current crew assignments. Depreciation is explicitly recorded, without automatic schedules.

The development journal reconstructs available historical sales, invoices, physical movements, payments, contractual salaries and operating costs. Initial stock costs use receipt evidence or labeled consistent test estimates; vehicle estimates and initial capital are explicit, with no arbitrary profit backfill. Empty earlier periods for new businesses report zero. Backdated sources extend history and retain their proper accounting dates. Approximate buying-price overrides remain separate from valuation. Monthly commissions revalue their earned acquisition batches and subsequent FIFO consumption. Valid monthly savepoints plus indexed subsequent journal activity return summarized positions; audit headers and expanded lines have separate bounded RPC pages. Trusted Finance membership and explicit OWNER analysis-sharing grants are checked per request.

Product photos retain the shared `items.image_url` reference and `product-images` Storage bucket. The reference stores a tenant-prefixed object path, validated against Storage; the bucket is private. `useProductImages.ts` owns uploads, cached signed reads and replacement/removal. Uploads normalize to bounded WebP rasters before Storage; server policies enforce trusted tenancy, capabilities, MIME types and size limits. Catalogs, POS, purchases and sales reuse `ProductImage`.
### FIFO and monthly LPG commissions

The canonical implementation is migration `20261031221903_monthly_commission_fifo.sql`. Private FIFO acquisition layers and consumption links maintain current cost pools; journals and month-end savepoints supply historical reports. `useMonthlyCommissions` and the LPG costing panel manage protected Package/Refill monthly pairs and attribution/history. The inventory RPC projects one active pair into both the displayed rates and approximate buying prices; restore-automatic uses that same private reference. Historical FIFO continues to use exact receipt-month rates. Purchases record actual receipt dates and earned-month deductions independently of their own gross acquisition costs. `PurchaseCommission` provides owner corrections through the same purchase audit and recovery conventions. Filled packages include their shell; package sales no longer consume a separate empty shell. FIFO refill shell costs move through transit and bottle receivables exactly once.

Finance navigation is Analysis, Wallet Ledger and Loans. `BalanceAdjustments` integrates opening cash, owner investments, corrections and existing asset/obligation registers without duplicating histories. Customer dues predicates run before directory pagination and use current monetary/bottle balances independently of activity-date filters.

## Financial dimensions

`financial_types` defines system/custom operation identity; fixed `accounting_accounts` determine recognition. Every `accounting_lines` row has type, business and original journal source, with a separate consolidated amount for internal transport eliminations. `wallet_transactions` is the only cash ledger; `wallets.business_balances` projects three signed portions atomically. Custom source records live in `financial_entries`, and asset/loan/obligation/payroll sources retain ownership allocations. Existing FIFO layers/consumptions also carry internal transport overhead; no second inventory issue algorithm is used.

The current contract is [Financial operation matrix](modules/financial-type-matrix.md). ## Platform and business access

`#/admin` is a separately guarded platform workspace; `#/users` is OWNER account/access management. `AuthContext` uses `get_access_context` for verified session, status, workspace and capability state. Business selection lives in a private Auth-session record, while business actor foreign keys use `tenant_actors` so platform writes retain the real administrator without a fabricated membership. OWNER always has every capability; staff role defaults may be replaced by validated individual allowlists. Public signup and `setup_business` are removed. [Authorization](../supabase/authorization.md) owns the detailed session/RLS/RPC, password, Storage and audit contracts.

## Centralized disaster recovery

`deploy/backup` owns pgBackRest physical cluster backups/local WAL, sealed Storage/configuration captures, Restic encryption/deduplication and nightly rclone transfers. A shared deployment lock and a short archive lock separate live writes from immutable captures. Cloud snapshots retain their complete dependencies for 30 daily days and 12 explicitly tagged monthly full points. The root-owned Unix controller independently enforces current superadmin Auth/session checks behind the existing private account service. `useBackups` owns the frontend contract; `BackupManagement` renders cached statistics and schedules/queues operations without daytime Drive polling. Web OAuth uses a fixed HTTPS callback and backend-only refresh tokens. Monthly drills restore into new internal-network containers with empty temporary PGDATA, disabled cron and no public ports. Recovery secrets are excluded from captures and exported separately. See the [recovery procedure](../deploy/backup/recovery.md).
