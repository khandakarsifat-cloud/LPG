# Purchases and logistics

[Architecture](../architecture.md) · [Stock rules](sales-and-inventory.md) · [Authorization](../../supabase/authorization.md)

Purchase lists display three rows based on the actual grid columns, with at least ten cards in narrow containers rounded to whole rows. `useBatchedDirectory` caches server batches of 25 per filter/sort scope, then slices the requested display page; cached navigation and column changes do not cause another request. Missing batches are fetched on demand, and a direct page jump requests only the batches covering that page. Equal columns preserve card widths, and normal page scrolling reaches all rows. Capacity changes preserve the first visible record within the new page. `get_purchase_directory` applies filters, AND/OR search, inclusive Dhaka dates and sorting before backend pagination, then builds products and operating costs only for the batch. `get_purchase_overview` calculates business-wide financial summaries from purchase headers without loading every order's lines. Only the bounded directory/by-ID APIs remain; the unused `list_purchases` endpoint is removed.

`get_purchases_by_ids` accepts at most 100 IDs. Inventory history requests only referenced purchases on its current page, plus any open purchase. An open detail dialog separately refreshes its selected purchase, so saving or filtering an order out of the current page cannot strand its details. Purchase mutations invalidate directory, overview and detail caches together; dirty forms retain their existing revision checks.

Fleet cards retain twelve desktop/four phone records. Vehicle and transit directories use server-side search, filters and bounded batches. Details load independently by ID, so a save or filter change cannot strand an open dialog. Assignment and payment histories have bounded server pages. The transit directory combines purchase/custom journeys and vehicle service trips before filtering and pagination. Service trips use their service date, include expense-category search and Open/Finalized status badges, and open their existing service details. Employee histories include only journeys with recorded crew. Dates are inclusive Bangladesh dates with deterministic date/ID sorting.

The directory defaults to both LPG and Stove; its Business filter also scopes the overview. Switching business retains the other filters. New Purchase uses the selected business, or asks LPG/Stove when both are visible. Sort by selects purchase date, total cost, outstanding money or supplier name, with an independent direction button matching Customers. Counts, sort order and category filtering remain server-side before pagination.

## Purchase workflow

The plant determines the supplying company (`lpg_brands`). Plant/company remain fixed after creation; products must belong to that company. Historical records retain their original lines and billing evidence.

| Action | Inventory and vehicle | Money and editing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create | Plant-only drafts are allowed. No vehicle reservation or stock movement. | No wallet posting. Vehicle, products, expected quantities and financials remain editable. |
| Save draft | Check all required-empty shortages against current stock, grouped by SKU. Saving never reserves empties. | Save changes keeps the detail modal open. A shortage popup lists every affected product with required, available and missing quantities; Okay returns to the unchanged form. |
| Dispatch | Requires a vehicle at Home with an active Driver assignment and valid lines. Deduct Gas quantity minus Bottle quantity; retain their FIFO value in custody. Create an active transit and reserve the vehicle. | Retain the transport fee as an internal allocation, excluded from recorded revenue and profit. Prices and costs remain editable until the first normal payment; notes keep their existing editing rules. Products, vehicle and quantities lock. |
| Receive | Always receive the saved expected quantities. Add Gas quantity as filled cylinders with linked Gas/Bottle component sources, mark the transit Returned and release the vehicle. Final transit completion confirms and pays every remaining crew and operating cost from Cash Register. | No receipt quantity inputs or discrepancy workflow. Receipt records the actual business-local date . Receipt does not deduct transit expenses. Financials remain editable until receipt or the first normal payment. |
| Payment | After dispatch, independently of receipt; no stock/vehicle effects. | Partial or full payments across active wallets. Additional payments settle the outstanding balance. Each wallet payment settles the parent purchase; company and labour remain a visible breakdown. Financial values lock after receipt or the first normal payment. |
| Initiate cancellation | OWNER only, Created or In Transit; Received purchases can never be cancelled. Exact `CANCEL` confirmation returns recorded dispatched empties, cancels linked transits and releases the vehicle if it has no other active assignment. | Stops all normal purchase operations and creates immutable obligations from original ledger evidence. No automatic refund. Incurred transit expenses remain intact. |
| Refund | Cancelled purchases with outstanding obligations; no inventory/vehicle effects. | Partial refunds across multiple transactions and active destination wallets. Refund only actual source-backed purchase payments. Only full settlement of every obligation sets `financial_closed_at`. Transit operating-cost closure remains independent. |

The purchase filter bar shares its slate palette and field styling with inventory history while retaining purchase search, statuses, date range and sorting. Inventory history can open this same purchase modal through a saved movement reference; existing purchase read/manage permissions still apply.

Received + Paid purchases show their locked acquisition summary and activity history; completion also locks the rebate settlement. Received LPG purchases cannot be cancelled, even when unpaid. Stove cancellation uses the guarded reversal below. Cancelled purchases cannot be edited or restarted. Saving, payment, dispatch, receipt and cancellation retain the detail modal. Warning dialogs preserve unsaved values. Product cards display current Filled/Empty stock beneath the selected product.

Each LPG SKU has one line with Gas quantity G > 0, Bottle quantity 0 ≤ B ≤ G and separate unit prices. Drafts reserve nothing. Dispatch requires G − B owned empties, checked under ordered SKU locks. It reduces on-hand Bottle and moves the exact available slices into supplier holdings while keeping the original acquisitions. Receipt adds G Gas and G Bottle to on-hand stock. It closes the holdings, adds a Gas acquisition and creates a Bottle acquisition for the B new bottles if any. Cancelling before receipt releases the same holdings back to empty stock at their original cost and age.

`subtotal = Σ(G × gas unit price + B × bottle unit price)`; `company_payable = subtotal`; `payable_amount = subtotal + labour`; `total_cost = subtotal + labour + transport`. Stove uses its ordinary product quantities/prices. Amounts are finite, nonnegative exact cents. Labour and internal transport are allocated by component invoice value using cumulative cent boundaries. The original shell basis is transferred separately, never purchased again. Transport is retained in buyer Supplies and subsequent COGS, including Combined reporting. The optional owner-entered Commission rebate used (Tk) is recorded separately from these gross amounts. `outstanding = payable_amount − rebate_amount − paid_amount`; `paid_amount` contains actual wallet payments only.

`payment_status` is Unpaid, Part paid or Paid; receiving does not depend on it. The header overview reports remaining total cost, and cancelled transactions have separate Pending refund / Financially closed filters.

Normal payments append `purchase_payments` and referenced `wallet_transactions`; the single Finance posting trigger changes balances. Partial/multi-wallet payments settle invoice and labour obligations; internal transport moves no cash. Product quantities lock at dispatch. Financial values lock at receipt or first normal payment, preventing repricing a settled invoice. Notes retain their existing rules.

### Company rebate settlement

The purchase form has one manual `rebate_amount`, default zero; there are no monthly rates or product/component entitlements. Created drafts create no rebate income. Dispatch or direct Stove receipt recognizes the confirmed application with the invoice. An owner may amend the amount through `set_purchase_rebate` while the purchase is incomplete, without unlocking prices or quantities. Receipt plus full settlement permanently locks the commission rebate in the UI, RPC and table guard. A retry of an already successful request still returns its original result. Values are finite exact cents, capped by the company product subtotal and remaining gross purchase balance. Revision checks and the shared recoverable request ID reject changed retries.

Gross invoice value, labour/transport allocation, selling prices, automatic/manual buying references, physical stock, FIFO and COGS remain unchanged. A new rebate debits supplier payable and credits `other_income`, classified by the fixed Company rebate system type and purchasing business. It changes overall business profit, not sale-level COGS or product margins. This is the explicitly selected management-reporting treatment; an inventory purchase-price rebate may require different statutory treatment under IAS 2.

`app_private.purchase_rebate_events` is immutable source evidence; journals remain the financial reporting authority. Company credit is derived from awarded income less applied amounts, serialized within the same trusted tenant/company/business. LPG uses the immutable brand identity; Stove uses its normalized fixed supplier name. The UI adds no separate credit choices: a later entered rebate consumes available company credit first and recognizes only the additional award as new income. Do not also enter this amount as a custom Revenue entry.

Before completion, reducing an erroneous amount reverses its newly recognized income first and restores any previously consumed credit. Cancelling a genuine applied purchase releases its rebate into company credit while retaining the confirmed award; only original wallet debits enter cash refund obligations. Company credit is visible in purchase settlement and Finance receivables, not a wallet balance. It is not automatically spent unless a rebate amount is entered on a subsequent purchase. Cancelled source rebates stay locked. A company withdrawing an already confirmed award is not represented by pretending a cash refund occurred.

### Cancellation and refund accounting

Cancellation records OWNER confirmation and initiation in one atomic transaction, returns original dispatched empties, and preserves original payments and movements. A source-backed parent purchase refund obligation comes from actual recorded external debits; no assumed refund is created for unattributed historical paid headers or internal transport allocations. Partial, multi-wallet refunds may return to different active wallets. Request recovery, purchase locking, immutable payment/ledger references and financial closure prevent duplicates or over-refunds. Incurred transit expenses remain payable independently.

Cancellation restores the exact original dispatched shell layers and acquisition chronology, preserves payments and creates only evidenced refund obligations. A cancellation is not an inventory purchase or automatic cash refund.


## Vehicles, assignments and transits

`vehicles` is the canonical fleet table; `vehicle_id` is shared by purchases and transits. Name, serial number and informational capacity are the only required creation fields. Size and manual location fields and the old truck RPCs are removed. Location is derived from an active journey's destination, otherwise Home. Capacity never limits inventory quantities or departure.

`vehicle_assignments` records each Driver/optional Helper link, its name snapshot, actor and start/end times. One current employee assignment per vehicle role and one current vehicle per employee are enforced by partial unique indexes. `save_vehicle` serializes assignment changes, checks current employment/role, records unlink/reassignment history and uses revision plus request-ID guards. Unlink another vehicle before assigning its employee. Vehicle edits affect future departures only.

At departure, an insert trigger copies the current employee IDs and names onto the transit. A Driver is mandatory and the Helper is optional. Later role, employment or vehicle changes do not rewrite those participants. Existing historical transits retain unknown crew when evidence is unavailable; only uniquely identified earlier payment evidence can attribute a historical participant. Current assignments are never guessed for old journeys.

Purchase destination/client come from the plant/company. A custom transit requires a client, destination and vehicle at Home with a Driver. Starting confirms and collects the full fee into Cash Register through the canonical operating-payment ledger, in the same transaction as departure. Request-ID retries never collect twice. Creation does not rewrite earlier journeys or assume historical collections.

- **Complete transit:** One confirmation shows the remaining Driver, Helper and other costs, all paid from Cash Register. Unsaved cost edits, canonical employee payments, operating payments, closure and vehicle return commit atomically. Existing partial payments count at their net amounts, so only the remainder is paid. Cost and payment capabilities remain enforced; stale revisions reject before writes. Exact completion retries cannot pay twice; conflicting revisions or changed completion costs reject. An open older custom journey with an uncollected fee explicitly confirms its remaining Cash Register collection at completion. Closed legacy journeys with receivables remain collectible through the existing client settlement route; closing does not forgive debt.
- **Vehicle return:** Custom journeys return Home through completion; there is no separate Return vehicle Home action. Purchase receipt continues to release the vehicle before cost completion. Purchase journeys must first be received or cancelled in Purchases. Completing an older journey cannot release a vehicle on a newer active journey.
- **Payment evidence:** Driver/helper amounts require the recorded participants and cannot fall below net payments. Canonical employee entries appear in Employee and Finance history independently of salary/bonus. Other expenses retain configured categories and their operating-payment evidence. Payment history/adjustments remain collapsed in the transit modal; service trips keep their independent expense/advance settlement and finalization.
- **Cancel transit:** Custom cancellation removes earned revenue and releases the vehicle. Actual collected fees require recorded client refunds before cost completion. Incurred employee earnings and operating expenses remain payable; cancellation never invents a refund or employee return. A cancelled journey retains that status when its costs are finalized. Purchase cancellation/refunds retain their existing source-backed workflow.

Vehicle details combine current crew/location, operational and financial summaries, assignment changes and complete transit history. Logistics and per-vehicle profit use the same recognition journals as Finance: external transport revenue less transit and service expenses. Internal purchase transport is Logistics business revenue retained in additive Combined reporting; shared expenses retain their recorded business allocations. Per-transit details show recorded profit, with proposed cost edits explicitly labeled until saved. Employee Activity history includes transit participation and vehicle assignment/unlink entries, selectable with the activity dropdown. Vehicle assignment history has no filters. Transit details summarize canonical paid amounts and expose payment history/adjustments in a collapsed section; canonical Employee/Finance ledger evidence remains unchanged. Inside Vehicle details, transit history uses compact clickable entries that open the transit detail dialog; advanced filters collapse behind Filters. Histories filter and paginate at the database; no all-transit browser download remains.

Gas plants and area officers are maintained in Settings. Purchases continue to read the same plant IDs and brand relationships. The separate Gas Plants route/navigation is removed.

## Integrity and authorization

All mutations derive actor and tenant on the server. Purchase actions require `purchases:manage`; cancellation additionally requires trusted OWNER membership and exact `CANCEL`; transit cost/workflow actions require `logistics:manage`. Read permissions remain separate. Direct browser ledger, stock, purchase and transit writes remain denied. New RPCs use the established application owner, empty search paths and explicit grants; private helpers are not browser callable.

Purchase revisions and transit cost revisions reject stale requests. Purchase creation uses tenant/request idempotency and saves its original payload for future requests; reusing a key with changed details is rejected. Older request rows remain untouched. The browser persists an uncertain creation request per tenant/user in session storage; Recover purchase replays that original request after a response loss or modal/page reload. Payments, cancellation and refunds use the shared `finance_requests` registry, tenant/request advisory locks, stored actor/payload/results, and session-storage recovery. Matching retries return the original response before checking later lifecycle state; changed payloads reject. Dispatch, receipt and transit closure retain their transition/revision protection. Retired full-payment and immediate-cancellation APIs are removed; only current independent settlement contracts remain. Purchase → vehicle → transit → ordered employees/items → ordered wallets is the shared lock order where these resources are needed. Vehicle assignment is serialized; active trips cannot overlap. Cost saves and lifecycle mutations invalidate purchases, inventory, logistics, wallets and Dashboard as appropriate.

Purchase activity stores one server-generated message with its purchase, actor and timestamp. Edits describe changed fields and product lines without storing full revisions or note text. Unchanged saves add no activity. Creation, dispatch, receipt, cancellation and other useful milestones use the same format. Payment and refund cards come directly from purchase payment records grouped by operation, with linked wallet legs visible to users who have History access for those wallets. The detail modal shows six chronological entries per page through the bounded history RPC. Cancellation and refunds preserve the original ledger evidence.

## Vehicle service trips

Vehicle details exposes Service trips beside Assignments. Each trip groups up to 100 categorized expenses, a title and service date. Settings maintains Engine Oil, Spare Parts, Trip Cost, Road Permit Renewal, Vehicle Tax, Other Costs and custom categories. Saved entries snapshot category names; archived categories retain history. Expenses can be added, edited or removed until finalization, with immutable before/after activity. The directory applies search, category and Bangladesh date filters before server pagination. Transit history also filters incurred expense categories.

Service payments reference the entire trip, never its individual expense components. Any active wallet can fund partial/split payments or an advance. Net payments above incurred costs remain unapplied advances; returns reference an original payment and credit its original wallet, capped at the unreturned amount. All outstanding costs and surplus advances must be settled before finalization. Finalizing locks expenses and payments without posting another expense or debit.

`operating_cost_events` captures incurred category costs and corrections. `operating_payments` records actual wallet settlements. Vehicle operating summaries and Finance count service costs once, independently of payment timing. [Financial analysis](financial-analysis.md) defines additive business reporting and transaction-time valuation.

## Stove purchases and vehicle lifecycle

Purchases switches between LPG and Stove. Stove orders share catalog, partial/multi-wallet payments, credit and refunds. Direct receipt is default; positive internal transport requires the same vehicle/crew workflow. Dispatch records a noncash Stove/Logistics allocation once. Receipt and completion do not debit a wallet. Receipt or payment freezes invoice values/quantities. Received Stove cancellation requires original quantity and carrying-value reversibility. Performed internal delivery remains earned; returned stock expenses its original transport overhead, retained in Combined COGS. Unreceived cancellation reverses the allocation. Actual original payments create staged refund obligations; no refund is invented.

Every vehicle has explicit book value/funding at creation and an independent `active`, `out_of_service` or `retired` availability. Existing journeys may return while out of service; new assignments are rejected in the backend. Retirement requires active journeys to return, permanently blocks new departures and preserves all old activity. Owners sell or dismantle through the shared asset operation, recording proceeds and expenses in a real wallet. Disposal gain/loss is net proceeds less remaining book value. Explicit depreciation is manual; existing development values are labeled estimates. Equipment and credit-funded assets use the same book/obligation model.

## Verification and deployment

Purchase dispatch, receipt, rebates, payments and refunds join the shared action collector: each actual financial action/date posts once, combining equivalent product-level effects. Buyer transport remains Supplies and subsequent COGS, with separate Logistics revenue retained in additive Combined results. Immediate Logistics recognition/settlement removes zero-net clearing lines; later crew, operating and service settlements remain independent actions. Purchase items, custody/FIFO links, rebate events and operating events/payments retain detailed evidence. See the [Finance posting contract](finance.md) and [measured verification](../audits/summarized-accounting-2026-11-05.md).

The [accounting guide](financial-analysis.md) defines current financial recognition, stock carrying values and verification commands. Existing historical migration suites remain at their owning replay boundary; current rollback SQL, concurrent transactions and real Auth/published-browser fixtures exercise the unified model. Deploy pending forward migrations before the dependent frontend using the established VPS/Cloudflare workflow; never rewrite applied migrations or alter Azure.
