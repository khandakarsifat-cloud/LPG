# Purchases and logistics

[Architecture](../architecture.md) · [Stock rules](sales-and-inventory.md) · [Authorization](../../supabase/authorization.md)

Purchase lists display three rows based on the actual grid columns, with at least ten cards in narrow containers rounded to whole rows. `useBatchedDirectory` caches server batches of 25 per filter/sort scope, then slices the requested display page; cached navigation and column changes do not cause another request. Missing batches are fetched on demand, and a direct page jump requests only the batches covering that page. Equal columns preserve card widths, and normal page scrolling reaches all rows. Capacity changes preserve the first visible record within the new page. `get_purchase_directory` applies filters, AND/OR search, inclusive Dhaka dates and sorting before backend pagination, then builds products and operating costs only for the batch. `get_purchase_overview` calculates business-wide financial summaries from purchase headers without loading every order's lines. Only the bounded directory/by-ID APIs remain; the unused `list_purchases` endpoint is removed.

`get_purchases_by_ids` accepts at most 100 IDs. Inventory history requests only referenced purchases on its current page, plus any open purchase. An open detail dialog separately refreshes its selected purchase, so saving or filtering an order out of the current page cannot strand its details. Purchase mutations invalidate directory, overview and detail caches together; dirty forms retain their existing revision checks.

Fleet cards retain twelve desktop/four phone records. Vehicle and transit directories use server-side search, filters and bounded batches. Details load independently by ID, so a save or filter change cannot strand an open dialog. Assignment and payment histories have bounded server pages. Transits have inclusive Bangladesh date filters, journey/status filters and deterministic date/ID sorting.

The directory defaults to both LPG and Stove; its Business filter also scopes the overview. Switching business retains the other filters. New Purchase uses the selected business, or asks LPG/Stove when both are visible. Sort by selects purchase date, total cost, outstanding money or supplier name, with an independent direction button matching Customers. Counts, sort order and category filtering remain server-side before pagination.

## Purchase workflow

The plant determines the supplying company (`lpg_brands`). Plant/company remain fixed after creation; products must belong to that company. Historical records retain their original lines and billing evidence.

| Action | Inventory and vehicle | Money and editing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create | Plant-only drafts are allowed. No vehicle reservation or stock movement. | No wallet posting. Vehicle, products, expected quantities and financials remain editable. |
| Save draft | Check all refill shortages against current stock, grouped by SKU. Saving never reserves empties. | Save changes keeps the detail modal open. A shortage popup lists every affected product with required, available and missing quantities; Okay returns to the unchanged form. |
| Dispatch | Requires a vehicle at Home with an active Driver assignment and valid lines. Deduct one empty per expected refill; new-cylinder lines send none. Create an active transit and reserve the vehicle. | Retain the transport fee as an internal allocation, excluded from recorded revenue and profit. Prices, commission and costs remain editable until the first normal payment; notes keep their existing editing rules. Products, vehicle and quantities lock. |
| Receive | Always receive the saved expected quantities. Add filled cylinders with separate refill/new movement references, mark the transit Returned and release the vehicle. Final transit completion requires every incurred crew and operating cost to be settled. | No receipt quantity inputs or discrepancy workflow. Receipt records the actual business-local date and, for a commission deduction, its earned month (default previous month). Receipt does not deduct transit expenses. Financials remain editable until receipt or the first normal payment. |
| Payment | After dispatch, independently of receipt; no stock/vehicle effects. | Partial or full payments across active wallets. Additional payments settle the outstanding balance. Each wallet payment settles the parent purchase; company and labour remain a visible breakdown. Financial values lock after receipt or the first normal payment. |
| Initiate cancellation | OWNER only, Created or In Transit; Received purchases can never be cancelled. Exact `CANCEL` confirmation returns recorded dispatched empties, cancels linked transits and releases the vehicle if it has no other active assignment. | Stops all normal purchase operations and creates immutable obligations from original ledger evidence. No automatic refund. Incurred transit expenses remain intact. |
| Refund | Cancelled purchases with outstanding obligations; no inventory/vehicle effects. | Partial refunds across multiple transactions and active destination wallets. Refund only actual source-backed purchase payments. Only full settlement of every obligation sets `financial_closed_at`. Transit operating-cost closure remains independent. |

The purchase filter bar shares its slate palette and field styling with inventory history while retaining purchase search, statuses, date range and sorting. Inventory history can open this same purchase modal through a saved movement reference; existing purchase read/manage permissions still apply.

Received + Paid purchases show a read-only summary and activity history. Received LPG purchases cannot be cancelled, even when unpaid. Stove cancellation uses the guarded reversal below. Cancelled purchases cannot be edited or restarted. Saving, payment, dispatch, receipt and cancellation retain the detail modal. Warning dialogs preserve unsaved values. Inline refill availability becomes red when requested quantity exceeds available empties.

Each SKU can have one refill and one new-cylinder line. Quantities are positive whole numbers. The server derives outgoing empties, checks save/dispatch shortages under ordered item locks and reports every shortage as structured error detail. Dispatch checks again because saving a draft does not reserve stock. Already dispatched historical outgoing quantities remain unchanged.

`subtotal = sum(billed_quantity × unit_price)`; `company_payable = subtotal − commission`; `total_cost = company_payable + transport_cost + labour_cost`. Money must be finite, nonnegative and have at most two decimal places. Commission cannot exceed subtotal. New receipts use expected quantities; historical actual/billed quantities remain readable and are never rewritten. Wallets may go negative under the existing rules. `payable_amount = company_payable + labour_cost` is the generated external obligation. Transport stays in the operational allocation breakdown; the journal eliminates it from inventory cost and external revenue. Dispatch, receipt and cost edits never transfer or deduct wallet funds. Cash Register, bKash, Nagad, Primary Bank and active custom wallets share the POS `PaymentEntries` editor and validation. Every payment/refund selector contains real active wallets only. These header totals describe operational settlement. FIFO acquisition uses the gross invoice plus external labour, with dispatched shell cost transferred separately; internal transport is eliminated. Received deduction corrections retain original payments and append attributed changes.

`payment_status` is Unpaid, Part paid or Paid; receiving does not depend on it. The header overview reports remaining total cost, and cancelled transactions have separate Pending refund / Financially closed filters.

Normal payments append `purchase_payments` and referenced `wallet_transactions` rows; the existing Finance posting trigger alone changes balances. Commission reduces the cash payable by settling earlier earned supplier receivables. It never discounts this purchase's gross acquisition cost. Prices, products, commission and costs lock after the first normal payment, preventing repricing a partially settled invoice. Before that point, the internal transport allocation can be edited without cash effects. Notes retain their existing editing rules.

### Cancellation and refund accounting

Cancellation records OWNER confirmation and initiation in one atomic transaction, returns original dispatched empties, and preserves original payments and movements. A source-backed parent purchase refund obligation comes from actual recorded external debits; no assumed refund is created for unattributed historical paid headers or internal transport allocations. Partial, multi-wallet refunds may return to different active wallets. Request recovery, purchase locking, immutable payment/ledger references and financial closure prevent duplicates or over-refunds. Incurred transit expenses remain payable independently.

The accounting migration preserves original virtual-wallet and ledger rows in an immutable archive, corrects only evidenced automatic real-account funding and linked virtual-funding refunds, and removes obsolete virtual relationships. Unknown original real-wallet attribution stays unknown. Original purchase headers and refund obligations are archived before normalization. Purchase history exposes archived evidence without treating it as present wallet cash. Individual real-wallet postings and payment snapshots remain auditable.


## Vehicles, assignments and transits

`vehicles` is the canonical fleet table; `vehicle_id` is shared by purchases and transits. Name, serial number and informational capacity are the only required creation fields. Size and manual location fields and the old truck RPCs are removed. Location is derived from an active journey's destination, otherwise Home. Capacity never limits inventory quantities or departure.

`vehicle_assignments` records each Driver/optional Helper link, its name snapshot, actor and start/end times. One current employee assignment per vehicle role and one current vehicle per employee are enforced by partial unique indexes. `save_vehicle` serializes assignment changes, checks current employment/role, records unlink/reassignment history and uses revision plus request-ID guards. Unlink another vehicle before assigning its employee. Vehicle edits affect future departures only.

At departure, an insert trigger copies the current employee IDs and names onto the transit. A Driver is mandatory and the Helper is optional. Later role, employment or vehicle changes do not rewrite those participants. Existing historical transits retain unknown crew when evidence is unavailable; only uniquely identified earlier payment evidence can attribute a historical participant. Current assignments are never guessed for old journeys.

Purchase destination/client come from the plant/company. A custom transit requires a client, destination and vehicle at Home with a Driver. Creation reserves the vehicle and records its revenue without wallet posting. Actual client collections and refunds use recoverable partial/multi-wallet operations against real accounts. Driver/helper amounts saved in transit details are deferred employee earnings: saving them does not debit a wallet. Costs require a recorded participant and cannot fall below net payments. Other operating expenses use the configured vehicle expense categories and remain part of the same transit obligation.

- **Return vehicle Home:** Custom journeys can return and release the vehicle before crew settlement. The transit remains Returned with editable costs and outstanding earnings. Purchase receipt performs this same operational return.
- **Pay driver/helper:** Pay the participant recorded on that transit through `employee_payment_operation`. Partial or full payments, including split active-wallet methods, create canonical employee payment entries and exactly one wallet ledger debit per entry. These payments appear in Employee and Finance history and do not affect salary months or bonuses. Audited returns reopen earnings until final completion.
- **Complete transit:** Driver and Helper outstanding amounts must both be zero. Purchase journeys must first be received or cancelled in Purchases. Non-employee expenses are paid separately from any real wallet, with partial/split payments and auditable returns. Final completion requires those expenses and crew earnings to be paid, sets `closed_at`, and locks costs and payments without another wallet posting. Returned and Completed are distinct database statuses. A cancelled journey retains its cancellation status when its costs are finalized. Repeated completion cannot post twice, and an older journey cannot release a vehicle on a newer active journey.
- **Cancel transit:** Custom cancellation removes earned revenue and releases the vehicle. Record actual client refunds separately; collected amounts must be returned before final closure. Incurred employee earnings and operating expenses remain payable; cancellation does not invent an employee return. Purchase cancellation and source-backed purchase refunds retain their independent workflow.

Vehicle details combine current crew/location, operational and financial summaries, assignment changes and complete transit history. Logistics and per-vehicle profit use the same recognition journals as Finance: external transport revenue less transit and service expenses. Purchase transport allocations never count as revenue; shared business expenses remain unallocated. Per-transit details show recorded profit, with proposed cost edits explicitly labeled until saved. Employee Activity history includes transit participation and vehicle assignment/unlink entries, selectable with the activity dropdown. Vehicle assignment history has no filters. Transit details show paid and remaining crew totals without a separate payment-history section; canonical Employee/Finance ledger evidence remains unchanged. Inside Vehicle details, transit history uses compact clickable entries that open the transit detail dialog; advanced filters collapse behind Filters. Histories filter and paginate at the database; no all-transit browser download remains.

Gas plants and area officers are maintained in Settings. Purchases continue to read the same plant IDs and brand relationships. The separate Gas Plants route/navigation is removed.

## Integrity and authorization

All mutations derive actor and tenant on the server. Purchase actions require `purchases:manage`; cancellation additionally requires trusted OWNER membership and exact `CANCEL`; transit cost/workflow actions require `logistics:manage`. Read permissions remain separate. Direct browser ledger, stock, purchase and transit writes remain denied. New RPCs use the established application owner, empty search paths and explicit grants; private helpers are not browser callable.

Purchase revisions and transit cost revisions reject stale requests. Purchase creation uses tenant/request idempotency and saves its original payload for future requests; reusing a key with changed details is rejected. Older request rows remain untouched. The browser persists an uncertain creation request per tenant/user in session storage; Recover purchase replays that original request after a response loss or modal/page reload. Payments, cancellation and refunds use the shared `finance_requests` registry, tenant/request advisory locks, stored actor/payload/results, and session-storage recovery. Matching retries return the original response before checking later lifecycle state; changed payloads reject. Dispatch, receipt and transit closure retain their transition/revision protection. Retired full-payment and immediate-cancellation APIs are removed; only current independent settlement contracts remain. Purchase → vehicle → transit → ordered employees/items → ordered wallets is the shared lock order where these resources are needed. Vehicle assignment is serialized; active trips cannot overlap. Cost saves and lifecycle mutations invalidate purchases, inventory, logistics, wallets and Dashboard as appropriate.

Purchase activity records creation, edits, dispatch, expected receipt, payment, cancellation, cost changes and closure with actor/time. The detail modal shows six chronological entries per page in a responsive two-column history, requesting one extra entry to detect the next page. Edit summaries compare recorded purchase values and available earlier product snapshots on that page; saved product quantities/prices remain expandable when earlier values are unavailable. Historical amounts come from the activity record, not today's purchase. Cancellation adds reversal rows; it does not delete original ledger evidence.

## Vehicle service trips

Vehicle details exposes Service trips beside Assignments. Each trip groups up to 100 categorized expenses, a title and service date. Settings maintains Engine Oil, Spare Parts, Trip Cost, Road Permit Renewal, Vehicle Tax, Other Costs and custom categories. Saved entries snapshot category names; archived categories retain history. Expenses can be added, edited or removed until finalization, with immutable before/after activity. The directory applies search, category and Bangladesh date filters before server pagination. Transit history also filters incurred expense categories.

Service payments reference the entire trip, never its individual expense components. Any active wallet can fund partial/split payments or an advance. Net payments above incurred costs remain unapplied advances; returns reference an original payment and credit its original wallet, capped at the unreturned amount. All outstanding costs and surplus advances must be settled before finalization. Finalizing locks expenses and payments without posting another expense or debit.

`operating_cost_events` captures incurred category costs and corrections. `operating_payments` records actual wallet settlements. Vehicle operating summaries and Finance count service costs once, independently of payment timing. [Financial analysis](financial-analysis.md) defines consolidated elimination and transaction-time valuation.

## Stove purchases and vehicle lifecycle

Purchases switches between LPG and Stove Business. Stove orders use a supplier name, shared catalog lines and the existing partial/multi-wallet payment/refund infrastructure. Credit is supported before or after receipt. Direct receipt remains the default. A positive internal transport charge requires a vehicle and the same dispatch/crew/transit workflow as LPG; dispatch settles the Stove portion of the selected wallet into Logistics Cash Register exactly once. Receipt and transit completion never charge it again. Receipt and any payment freeze values/quantities. Owners may cancel received stove orders only when both their original quantities and receipt carrying values can be reversed; original cash payments create staged refund obligations, and applied earned supplier credits are restored. Performed internal transportation remains earned when received goods return; its original FIFO overhead becomes a Stove return cost and is eliminated in consolidated reporting. Cancelling an unreceived delivery restores its original wallet portions. No replacement price, reset or invented refund is used.

Every vehicle has explicit book value/funding at creation and an independent `active`, `out_of_service` or `retired` availability. Existing journeys may return while out of service; new assignments are rejected in the backend. Retirement requires active journeys to return, permanently blocks new departures and preserves all old activity. Owners sell or dismantle through the shared asset operation, recording proceeds and expenses in a real wallet. Disposal gain/loss is net proceeds less remaining book value. Explicit depreciation is manual; existing development values are labeled estimates. Equipment and credit-funded assets use the same book/obligation model.
