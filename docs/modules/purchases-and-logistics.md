# Purchases and logistics

[Architecture](../architecture.md) · [Stock rules](sales-and-inventory.md) · [Authorization](../../supabase/authorization.md)

Purchase lists display three rows based on the actual grid columns on desktop and ten cards per phone page. `useBatchedDirectory` caches server batches of 25 per filter/sort scope, then slices the requested display page; cached navigation and column changes do not cause another request. Missing batches are fetched on demand, and a direct page jump requests only the batches covering that page. Equal columns preserve card widths, and normal page scrolling reaches all rows. Capacity changes preserve the first visible record within the new page. `get_purchase_directory` applies filters, AND/OR search, inclusive Dhaka dates and sorting before backend pagination, then builds products and operating costs only for the batch. `get_purchase_overview` calculates business-wide financial summaries from purchase headers without loading every order's lines. The prior `list_purchases` contract remains available for compatibility; application screens use the bounded APIs.

`get_purchases_by_ids` accepts at most 100 IDs. Inventory history requests only referenced purchases on its current page, plus any open purchase. An open detail dialog separately refreshes its selected purchase, so saving or filtering an order out of the current page cannot strand its details. Purchase mutations invalidate directory, overview and detail caches together; dirty forms retain their existing revision checks.

Fleet and each transit group show twelve records per desktop page and four per phone page. On phones the fleet table becomes labeled records with location editing in view; its status summaries still count all trucks.

## Purchase workflow

The plant determines the supplying company (`lpg_brands`). Plant/company remain fixed after creation; products must belong to that company. Historical records retain their original lines and billing evidence.

| Action | Inventory and truck | Money and editing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create | Plant-only drafts are allowed. No truck reservation or stock movement. | No wallet posting. Truck, products, expected quantities and financials remain editable. |
| Save draft | Check all refill shortages against current stock, grouped by SKU. Saving never reserves empties. | Save changes keeps the detail modal open. A shortage popup lists every affected product with required, available and missing quantities; Okay returns to the unchanged form. |
| Dispatch | Requires an idle truck and valid lines. Deduct one empty per expected refill; new-cylinder lines send none. Create an active transit and reserve the truck. | Transfer the transport fee from dealership to logistics once. Prices, commission and costs remain editable until the first normal payment; notes keep their existing editing rules. Products, truck and quantities lock. |
| Receive | Always receive the saved expected quantities. Add filled cylinders with separate refill/new movement references, complete the transit and release the truck. | No receipt quantity inputs or discrepancy workflow. Receipt does not deduct transit expenses. Financials remain editable until the first normal payment. |
| Payment | After dispatch, independently of receipt; no stock/truck effects. | Partial or full payments across active wallets. Additional payments settle the outstanding balance. Company payable is allocated first, then labour and any historical transport balance. Financial values lock after the first normal payment. |
| Initiate cancellation | OWNER only, Created or In Transit; Received purchases can never be cancelled. Exact `CANCEL` confirmation returns recorded dispatched empties, cancels linked transits and releases the truck if it has no other active assignment. | Stops all normal purchase operations and creates immutable obligations from original ledger evidence. No automatic refund. Incurred transit expenses remain intact. |
| Refund | Cancelled purchases with outstanding obligations; no inventory/truck effects. | Partial refunds across multiple transactions and active destination wallets. Transport funding reversals debit Logistics atomically. Only full settlement of every obligation sets `financial_closed_at`. Transit operating-cost closure remains independent. |

The purchase filter bar shares its slate palette and field styling with inventory history while retaining purchase search, statuses, date range and sorting. Inventory history can open this same purchase modal through a saved movement reference; existing purchase read/manage permissions still apply.

Received + Paid purchases show a read-only summary and activity history. Received purchases cannot be cancelled, even when unpaid. Cancelled purchases cannot be edited or restarted. Saving, payment, dispatch, receipt and cancellation retain the detail modal. Warning dialogs preserve unsaved values. Inline refill availability becomes red when requested quantity exceeds available empties.

Each SKU can have one refill and one new-cylinder line. Quantities are positive whole numbers. The server derives outgoing empties, checks save/dispatch shortages under ordered item locks and reports every shortage as structured error detail. Dispatch checks again because saving a draft does not reserve stock. Already dispatched historical outgoing quantities remain unchanged.

`subtotal = sum(billed_quantity × unit_price)`; `company_payable = subtotal − commission`; `total_cost = company_payable + transport_cost + labour_cost`. Money must be finite, nonnegative and have at most two decimal places. Commission cannot exceed subtotal. New receipts use expected quantities; historical actual/billed quantities remain readable and are never rewritten. Wallets may go negative under the existing rules. Dispatch still transfers transport funding from Primary Bank Account (the preserved `dealership` key) to Logistics. `paid_amount` includes this funding once; normal payments debit only the outstanding amount. Cash Register, bKash, Nagad, Primary Bank and active custom wallets share the POS `PaymentEntries` editor and validation. Logistics is excluded from payment/refund destination selectors. `payment_status` is Unpaid, Part paid or Paid; receiving does not depend on it. The header overview reports remaining total cost, and cancelled transactions have separate Pending refund / Financially closed filters.

Normal payments append `purchase_payments` and referenced `wallet_transactions` rows; the existing Finance posting trigger alone changes balances. Commission remains a company-price deduction. Prices, products, commission and costs lock after the first normal payment, preventing repricing a partially settled invoice. Before that point, transport-only funding can still be adjusted through the existing audited difference transfer. Notes retain their existing editing rules.

### Cancellation and refund accounting

Cancellation records OWNER confirmation and initiation in one atomic transaction, returns original dispatched empties, and preserves original payments/movements. Its separate `purchase_refund_obligations` identify company, labour/historical trip charges, transport, and any unclassified legacy purchase payments, with original ledger IDs, wallet labels, amounts and descriptions. Obligations come from actual recorded non-Logistics debits, never an assumed refund of an unattributed historic paid header. Historical Logistics credits without a corresponding purchasing debit become an explicit Logistics-only reversal, with no invented purchasing-business credit.

Each refund references one obligation and one recoverable operation ID, supports up to 20 positive two-decimal wallet amounts, and may return to different active wallets. Refund rows, destination credits and any corresponding Logistics funding debits commit together. Transport reversals are limited to recorded funding; they do not remove operating-cost entries. Purchase row locking prevents over-refunding across concurrent requests. `refund_due`, `refunded_amount` and `financial_closed_at` track pending and final closure. Old already-cancelled records retain their completed historical meaning; no historic money is reposted.

New wallet entries capture name/method snapshots; payment records preserve labels and references. Historical labels are captured at transition because earlier original names may be unavailable. The payment panel pages immutable ledger evidence separately from the existing six-entry activity history. Both include actor/time, component, operation and payment references. Cost edits capture before/after transit values; expense closure stays in Logistics.


## Transit workflow

[useLogistics](../../src/hooks/useLogistics.ts) owns gated reads/mutations. Transits have separate operational `status` (Active, Completed, Cancelled) and expense closure (`closed_at`). Neither purchase nor custom transits expose a status dropdown.

Purchase transit status is controlled exclusively by receiving/cancelling its purchase. Client, destination and description are populated from company, plant and purchase notes at dispatch. A custom transit requires a client name and idle truck; destination and description are optional. Its collected client transport fee credits logistics on creation.

- **Save changes:** Save driver, helper, oil, Others and named additional costs. Active and completed costs remain editable until closure. No wallet posting and no modal dismissal.
- **Complete transit:** Custom trips only. Free the truck; leave operating costs open. Purchase trips complete automatically on receipt.
- **Cancel transit:** Custom trips only, before closure. Refund the collected client fee from logistics once and free the truck. Incurred costs remain editable for review/closure. Purchase cancellation performs the corresponding linked-transit action.
- **Close transit:** Completed or Cancelled trips only. Explicit confirmation deducts saved operating costs from logistics once, sets `closed_at` and locks cost editing. Closing an older trip never releases a truck reserved by a newer trip.

Transport fees belong to purchase/client funding, while operating costs are logistics expenses. Transport-only purchase funding edits before the first normal payment transfer only the difference and synchronize the linked transit fee. A closed transit's fee cannot change. Normal payments never credit the fee twice. Historical income/expense evidence is recognized at migration time without posting money. Previously completed transits retain closed costs because the previous workflow already settled them; old received/active transits become completed with open costs and no automatic expense posting.

Cancellation reverses referenced inventory movements, or precisely identified historical purchase movement notes, rather than guessing stock from current expected lines. Refund amounts come from this purchase's recorded wallet transactions. Historical ledger rows, prices, receipt quantities, stock and wallet balances are preserved by migration. Existing spent transit costs are retained even when the purchase is later cancelled.

## Integrity and authorization

All mutations derive actor and tenant on the server. Purchase actions require `purchases:manage`; cancellation additionally requires trusted OWNER membership and exact `CANCEL`; transit cost/workflow actions require `logistics:manage`. Read permissions remain separate. Direct browser ledger, stock, purchase and transit writes remain denied. New RPCs use the established application owner, empty search paths and explicit grants; private helpers are not browser callable.

Purchase revisions and transit cost revisions reject stale requests. Purchase creation uses tenant/request idempotency and saves its original payload for future requests; reusing a key with changed details is rejected. Older request rows remain untouched. The browser persists an uncertain creation request per tenant/user in session storage; Recover purchase replays that original request after a response loss or modal/page reload. Payments, cancellation and refunds use the shared `finance_requests` registry, tenant/request advisory locks, stored actor/payload/results, and session-storage recovery. Matching retries return the original response before checking later lifecycle state; changed payloads reject. Dispatch, receipt and transit closure retain their transition/revision protection. Older cached full-payment and immediate-cancellation APIs reject with refresh guidance. Purchase → truck → transit → ordered items → ordered wallets is the shared lock order where these resources are needed. Truck assignment is serialized; active trips cannot overlap. Cost saves and lifecycle mutations invalidate purchases, inventory, logistics, wallets and Dashboard as appropriate.

Purchase activity records creation, edits, dispatch, expected receipt, payment, cancellation, cost changes and closure with actor/time. The detail modal shows six chronological entries per page in a responsive two-column history, requesting one extra entry to detect the next page. Edit summaries compare recorded purchase values and available earlier product snapshots on that page; saved product quantities/prices remain expandable when earlier values are unavailable. Historical amounts come from the activity record, not today's purchase. Cancellation adds reversal rows; it does not delete original ledger evidence.
