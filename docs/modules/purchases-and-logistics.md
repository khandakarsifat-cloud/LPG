# Purchases and logistics

[Architecture](../architecture.md) · [Stock rules](sales-and-inventory.md) · [Authorization](../../supabase/authorization.md)

Purchase lists display three rows based on the actual grid columns, with at least ten cards in narrow containers rounded to whole rows. `useBatchedDirectory` caches server batches of 25 per filter/sort scope, then slices the requested display page; cached navigation and column changes do not cause another request. Missing batches are fetched on demand, and a direct page jump requests only the batches covering that page. Equal columns preserve card widths, and normal page scrolling reaches all rows. Capacity changes preserve the first visible record within the new page. `get_purchase_directory` applies filters, AND/OR search, inclusive Dhaka dates and sorting before backend pagination, then builds products and operating costs only for the batch. `get_purchase_overview` calculates business-wide financial summaries from purchase headers without loading every order's lines. Only the bounded directory/by-ID APIs remain; the unused `list_purchases` endpoint is removed.

`get_purchases_by_ids` accepts at most 100 IDs. Inventory history requests only referenced purchases on its current page, plus any open purchase. An open detail dialog separately refreshes its selected purchase, so saving or filtering an order out of the current page cannot strand its details. Purchase mutations invalidate directory, overview and detail caches together; dirty forms retain their existing revision checks.

Fleet cards retain twelve desktop/four phone records. Vehicle and transit directories use server-side search, filters and bounded batches. Details load independently by ID, so a save or filter change cannot strand an open dialog. Assignment and payment histories have bounded server pages. Transits have inclusive Bangladesh date filters, journey/status filters and deterministic date/ID sorting.

## Purchase workflow

The plant determines the supplying company (`lpg_brands`). Plant/company remain fixed after creation; products must belong to that company. Historical records retain their original lines and billing evidence.

| Action | Inventory and vehicle | Money and editing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create | Plant-only drafts are allowed. No vehicle reservation or stock movement. | No wallet posting. Vehicle, products, expected quantities and financials remain editable. |
| Save draft | Check all refill shortages against current stock, grouped by SKU. Saving never reserves empties. | Save changes keeps the detail modal open. A shortage popup lists every affected product with required, available and missing quantities; Okay returns to the unchanged form. |
| Dispatch | Requires a vehicle at Home with an active Driver assignment and valid lines. Deduct one empty per expected refill; new-cylinder lines send none. Create an active transit and reserve the vehicle. | Transfer the transport fee from dealership to logistics once. Prices, commission and costs remain editable until the first normal payment; notes keep their existing editing rules. Products, vehicle and quantities lock. |
| Receive | Always receive the saved expected quantities. Add filled cylinders with separate refill/new movement references, mark the transit Returned and release the vehicle. Final transit completion requires paid crew earnings. | No receipt quantity inputs or discrepancy workflow. Receipt does not deduct transit expenses. Financials remain editable until the first normal payment. |
| Payment | After dispatch, independently of receipt; no stock/vehicle effects. | Partial or full payments across active wallets. Additional payments settle the outstanding balance. Company payable is allocated first, then labour and any historical transport balance. Financial values lock after the first normal payment. |
| Initiate cancellation | OWNER only, Created or In Transit; Received purchases can never be cancelled. Exact `CANCEL` confirmation returns recorded dispatched empties, cancels linked transits and releases the vehicle if it has no other active assignment. | Stops all normal purchase operations and creates immutable obligations from original ledger evidence. No automatic refund. Incurred transit expenses remain intact. |
| Refund | Cancelled purchases with outstanding obligations; no inventory/vehicle effects. | Partial refunds across multiple transactions and active destination wallets. Transport funding reversals debit Logistics atomically. Only full settlement of every obligation sets `financial_closed_at`. Transit operating-cost closure remains independent. |

The purchase filter bar shares its slate palette and field styling with inventory history while retaining purchase search, statuses, date range and sorting. Inventory history can open this same purchase modal through a saved movement reference; existing purchase read/manage permissions still apply.

Received + Paid purchases show a read-only summary and activity history. Received purchases cannot be cancelled, even when unpaid. Cancelled purchases cannot be edited or restarted. Saving, payment, dispatch, receipt and cancellation retain the detail modal. Warning dialogs preserve unsaved values. Inline refill availability becomes red when requested quantity exceeds available empties.

Each SKU can have one refill and one new-cylinder line. Quantities are positive whole numbers. The server derives outgoing empties, checks save/dispatch shortages under ordered item locks and reports every shortage as structured error detail. Dispatch checks again because saving a draft does not reserve stock. Already dispatched historical outgoing quantities remain unchanged.

`subtotal = sum(billed_quantity × unit_price)`; `company_payable = subtotal − commission`; `total_cost = company_payable + transport_cost + labour_cost`. Money must be finite, nonnegative and have at most two decimal places. Commission cannot exceed subtotal. New receipts use expected quantities; historical actual/billed quantities remain readable and are never rewritten. Wallets may go negative under the existing rules. Dispatch still transfers transport funding from Primary Bank Account (the preserved `dealership` key) to Logistics. `paid_amount` includes this funding once; normal payments debit only the outstanding amount. Cash Register, bKash, Nagad, Primary Bank and active custom wallets share the POS `PaymentEntries` editor and validation. Logistics is excluded from payment/refund destination selectors. `payment_status` is Unpaid, Part paid or Paid; receiving does not depend on it. The header overview reports remaining total cost, and cancelled transactions have separate Pending refund / Financially closed filters.

Normal payments append `purchase_payments` and referenced `wallet_transactions` rows; the existing Finance posting trigger alone changes balances. Commission remains a company-price deduction. Prices, products, commission and costs lock after the first normal payment, preventing repricing a partially settled invoice. Before that point, transport-only funding can still be adjusted through the existing audited difference transfer. Notes retain their existing editing rules.

### Cancellation and refund accounting

Cancellation records OWNER confirmation and initiation in one atomic transaction, returns original dispatched empties, and preserves original payments/movements. Its separate `purchase_refund_obligations` identify company, labour/historical trip charges, transport, and any unclassified legacy purchase payments, with original ledger IDs, wallet labels, amounts and descriptions. Obligations come from actual recorded non-Logistics debits, never an assumed refund of an unattributed historic paid header. Historical Logistics credits without a corresponding purchasing debit become an explicit Logistics-only reversal, with no invented purchasing-business credit.

Each refund references one obligation and one recoverable operation ID, supports up to 20 positive two-decimal wallet amounts, and may return to different active wallets. Refund rows, destination credits and any corresponding Logistics funding debits commit together. Transport reversals are limited to recorded funding; they do not remove operating-cost entries. Purchase row locking prevents over-refunding across concurrent requests. `refund_due`, `refunded_amount` and `financial_closed_at` track pending and final closure. Old already-cancelled records retain their completed historical meaning; no historic money is reposted.

New wallet entries capture name/method snapshots; payment records preserve labels and references. Historical labels are captured at transition because earlier original names may be unavailable. The payment summary retains balances and outstanding refund obligations. All chronological payment/refund evidence appears in Activity history: `get_purchase_activity` attaches each ledger row to one recorded operation/payment ID and retains unmatched historical ledger rows as independent events. Each signed amount, original wallet label, actor/time, component, ledger/payment/operation ID and reference remains available once. This is a read projection; no historical record or balance is rewritten. Cost edits capture before/after transit values; expense closure stays in Logistics.


## Vehicles, assignments and transits

`vehicles` is the canonical fleet table; `vehicle_id` is shared by purchases and transits. Name, serial number and informational capacity are the only required creation fields. Size and manual location fields and the old truck RPCs are removed. Location is derived from an active journey's destination, otherwise Home. Capacity never limits inventory quantities or departure.

`vehicle_assignments` records each Driver/optional Helper link, its name snapshot, actor and start/end times. One current employee assignment per vehicle role and one current vehicle per employee are enforced by partial unique indexes. `save_vehicle` serializes assignment changes, checks current employment/role, records unlink/reassignment history and uses revision plus request-ID guards. Unlink another vehicle before assigning its employee. Vehicle edits affect future departures only.

At departure, an insert trigger copies the current employee IDs and names onto the transit. A Driver is mandatory and the Helper is optional. Later role, employment or vehicle changes do not rewrite those participants. Existing historical transits retain unknown crew when evidence is unavailable; only uniquely identified earlier payment evidence can attribute a historical participant. Current assignments are never guessed for old journeys.

Purchase destination/client come from the plant/company. A custom transit requires a client, destination and vehicle at Home with a Driver. Creation reserves the vehicle and credits its actually collected client fee to Logistics once through `finance_requests` recovery. Driver/helper amounts saved in transit details are deferred employee earnings: saving them does not debit a wallet. Costs require a recorded participant and cannot fall below net payments. Other named operating expenses remain separate.

- **Return vehicle Home:** Custom journeys can return and release the vehicle before crew settlement. The transit remains Returned with editable costs and outstanding earnings. Purchase receipt performs this same operational return.
- **Pay driver/helper:** Pay the participant recorded on that transit through `employee_payment_operation`. Partial or full payments, including split active-wallet methods, create canonical employee payment entries and exactly one wallet ledger debit per entry. These payments appear in Employee and Finance history and do not affect salary months or bonuses. Audited returns reopen earnings until final completion.
- **Complete transit:** Driver and Helper outstanding amounts must both be zero. Purchase journeys must first be received or cancelled in Purchases. Final completion posts only non-employee operating costs to Logistics, sets `closed_at`, and locks costs and payments. Returned and Completed are distinct database statuses. A cancelled journey retains its cancellation status when its costs are finalized. Repeated completion cannot post twice, and an older journey cannot release a vehicle on a newer active journey.
- **Cancel transit:** Custom trips refund the collected client fee once and release the vehicle. Incurred employee earnings and operating expenses remain payable; cancellation does not invent an employee return. Purchase cancellation and source-backed purchase refunds retain their independent workflow.

Vehicle details combine current crew/location, operational and financial summaries, assignment changes and complete transit history. Employee Activity history includes transit participation and vehicle assignment/unlink entries, selectable with the activity dropdown. Vehicle assignment history has no filters. Transit details show paid and remaining crew totals without a separate payment-history section; canonical Employee/Finance ledger evidence remains unchanged. Inside Vehicle details, transit history uses compact clickable entries that open the transit detail dialog; advanced filters collapse behind Filters. Histories filter and paginate at the database; no all-transit browser download remains.

Gas plants and area officers are maintained in Settings. Purchases continue to read the same plant IDs and brand relationships. The separate Gas Plants route/navigation is removed.

## Integrity and authorization

All mutations derive actor and tenant on the server. Purchase actions require `purchases:manage`; cancellation additionally requires trusted OWNER membership and exact `CANCEL`; transit cost/workflow actions require `logistics:manage`. Read permissions remain separate. Direct browser ledger, stock, purchase and transit writes remain denied. New RPCs use the established application owner, empty search paths and explicit grants; private helpers are not browser callable.

Purchase revisions and transit cost revisions reject stale requests. Purchase creation uses tenant/request idempotency and saves its original payload for future requests; reusing a key with changed details is rejected. Older request rows remain untouched. The browser persists an uncertain creation request per tenant/user in session storage; Recover purchase replays that original request after a response loss or modal/page reload. Payments, cancellation and refunds use the shared `finance_requests` registry, tenant/request advisory locks, stored actor/payload/results, and session-storage recovery. Matching retries return the original response before checking later lifecycle state; changed payloads reject. Dispatch, receipt and transit closure retain their transition/revision protection. Retired full-payment and immediate-cancellation APIs are removed; only current independent settlement contracts remain. Purchase → vehicle → transit → ordered employees/items → ordered wallets is the shared lock order where these resources are needed. Vehicle assignment is serialized; active trips cannot overlap. Cost saves and lifecycle mutations invalidate purchases, inventory, logistics, wallets and Dashboard as appropriate.

Purchase activity records creation, edits, dispatch, expected receipt, payment, cancellation, cost changes and closure with actor/time. The detail modal shows six chronological entries per page in a responsive two-column history, requesting one extra entry to detect the next page. Edit summaries compare recorded purchase values and available earlier product snapshots on that page; saved product quantities/prices remain expandable when earlier values are unavailable. Historical amounts come from the activity record, not today's purchase. Cancellation adds reversal rows; it does not delete original ledger evidence.

It preserves vehicle/purchase/transit IDs, wallet/ledger amounts, stock, sales, purchase lines and employee payment evidence. Previously returned journeys with open costs normalize to Returned; historical finalized expenses remain finalized without reposting money.

Credentials stay in ignored/injected settings. Browser emulation does not establish physical Android/PWA or external payment-provider settlement.
