# Dashboard

`get_dashboard_snapshot` derives the trusted tenant from `dashboard:view`. It uses the server's Asia/Dhaka date, prepares the same contractual monthly payroll accruals as Finance for authorized Finance readers, then reads a stable statement snapshot. Seven daily buckets drive the four cards, yesterday comparisons and sparklines. No historical activity is fabricated; a zero comparison baseline displays “From zero” rather than an infinite percentage.

| Figure | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Today's Revenue | Net recognized consolidated income from canonical journals on the business date, excluding opening entries. Includes sales, settlement charges and other earned income; cancellation reversals reduce recognition on their accounting date. Dashboard readers without Finance permission see only product sales revenue. |
| Today's Expense | Consolidated journal expenses, including FIFO COGS and incurred/accrued costs. Inventory purchases, loan principal, transfers, equipment acquisitions and paying an already recognized obligation are not new expenses. Requires Finance permission. |
| Collected Amount | Actual `sale_payments` received on that business day, counting checkout and later collections once. Includes receipts against older invoices. Cancellation does not erase actual cash receipts; refunds remain separate outflows. Custom income, capital and transfers are not customer collections. |
| Total Sales | Completed sales created that day. Cancelled sales do not count. |

The Cash Register indicator uses the default wallet's immutable `type='cash'`, not an editable name or the sum of wallets. Other pages retain their combined balance indicator. All amounts use the existing BDT formatting.

Today's Sales returns the latest five completed invoices, with product descriptions and current settlement charges. Invoice IDs retain the application's saved UUID-prefix reference; no separate invoice sequence is invented. Rows and keyboard-accessible invoice buttons open existing transaction details. View All Sales applies the current day to the Sales directory.

LPG quantities sum `refill` and `package` lines across brands and customer tiers. Empty returns and cancelled sales are excluded. Active business-configured weights appear even with zero sales; a weight with actual sales remains visible after deactivation. Stove quantities group all sold products by their current product type. Active types appear even with zero units; inactive types remain visible when they have sales today. New businesses seed Single Stove, Double Stove, Regulator, Pipe and Others. Their stable system identities select dedicated icons even after a rename; custom types use a generic package icon with a UUID-assigned theme color that stays stable across refreshes. New, renamed or archived types invalidate the dashboard query. Horizontal scrolling stays inside the product strips; the sales table becomes compact records on phones.

Needs Attention uses the maintained customer dues projection, canonical per-sale outstanding bottle quantities and sellable inventory. Customer/Bottle Dues links apply existing customer directory filters. Stock alerts count products whose `filled_quantity < low_stock_threshold`, including zero stock. LPG empty-cylinder counts are separate inventory information. The per-product threshold defaults to five; zero disables the sellable-stock alert. Inventory → Stock Alerts pages/searches both catalogs and allows `inventory:manage` to change a threshold with an expected-value stale-edit guard. Changing thresholds never changes physical stock or financial evidence.

Recent Activity merges bounded, independently indexed reads of saved POS sales/cancellations, purchase lifecycle activity, later customer collections and financial entries. It uses recording timestamps, preserving the distinction from backdated accounting dates. Source records open the existing dialogs. Purchase and Finance events retain their underlying capability gates; dashboard-only users do not gain access to those sources or customer/stock aggregates. There is no persisted POS-open session stream, so opening POS is not presented as a fabricated event. View All opens paged activity.

Dashboard queries are tenant-keyed, refresh on entry/focus and poll every 30 seconds. Existing mutation invalidations update the dashboard after sales, collections, cancellation, stock and financial operations. Server queries aggregate date ranges instead of downloading transaction tables. Failures show an explicit retry and preserve an identified prior snapshot rather than displaying invented zeros.

Checks: `node supabase/tests/run-unified-accounting.mjs --vps dashboard.sql dashboard-stove-types.sql` uses normal RPC operations on a restored disposable database and reconciles the dashboard with independent operation amounts and Finance. `tests/dashboard.browser.cjs` covers intercepted responsive states, filters, thresholds and Ethernet configuration; `supabase/tests/dashboard-api.mjs --vps` covers real Auth/PostgREST and the published app using disposable businesses. Physical printer/Android/PWA acceptance remains separate; see [printing](../../print-bridge/README.md).

Manual purchase rebate income participates in the canonical recognized-income figures on its application/correction date. It creates no customer collection or wallet receipt. Product sales counts and FIFO/COGS remain unchanged.
