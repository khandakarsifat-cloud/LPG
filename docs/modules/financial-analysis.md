# Unified accounting and financial analysis

## Accounting boundary

`accounting_journals` and `accounting_lines` are the financial source of truth. Every journal balances signed debits and credits. Account classifications distinguish assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses. Append-only entries retain source, business date, recording time, actor and provenance. A deferred constraint validates each complete journal at commit; direct browser writes are denied.

Assets = Liabilities + Capital + Cumulative Revenue − Cumulative Expenses − Cumulative Owner Withdrawals, plus separately identified legitimate other equity movements. Accumulated income is cumulative revenue less cumulative expenses; it is already part of equity, never another independent equity account. Period income is closing accumulated income less opening accumulated income. It also equals the change in net assets after contributions, withdrawals and other equity movements. `get_finance_analysis` establishes contractual accruals, then reads all positions, totals and trends through one stable MVCC snapshot so concurrent backdated writes cannot mix financial states.

All monthly, yearly and custom positions use cumulative business-local cutoffs. A normal opening position ends immediately before the first selected day; closing includes the last selected day. Same-day supplemental opening evidence changes capital during that period without creating profit or operating cash flow. All-time uses the original opening entries at the earliest supported business date, then all subsequent history. Initial liabilities reduce opening capital. Authorized reporting may establish deterministic contractual salary accruals; it never authorizes discretionary activity in another business.

`app_private.sync_journal` posts changes to mutable source obligations using a locked source projection. It does not replace old journals. Wallet entries post cash and settle the relevant control account exactly once. `post_wallet_entry` remains the only function updating actual wallet balances. Cash transfers balance through one operation and produce zero business cash flow. Wallet corrections affect equity adjustments, not revenue. Additional capital and owner withdrawals have separate equity accounts.

## Recognition and inventory

Sales recognize revenue and customer receivables independently of collection. Full cancellation reverses the invoice and recorded stock carrying values; actual partial/multi-wallet refunds settle the customer credit separately. A sale without recorded carrying evidence must be reconciled before reversal; the development reconstruction supplies that evidence for historical movements. Sales retains a Cancelled / refunds filter for subsequent settlement.

`inventory_costs` is the current projection of private `fifo_layers` and `fifo_consumptions`. Each acquisition retains original quantity/value, remaining quantity/value, shell component and receipt chronology. Issues consume the oldest available layers; partial consumption and final rounding use the exact remaining value. Returns restore the original issued layers and their FIFO age. Immutable `inventory_cost_entries` and journals preserve original postings plus dated correction deltas. Reports read journals/savepoints and never replay purchases or choose a costing method by report duration.

Filled LPG units include gas and their cylinder shells. Spare empty cylinders are a separate asset. A package sale removes one filled package, with no additional empty-shell deduction. Refill dispatch moves the exact FIFO empty-shell cost into transit; receipt combines that shell with the new gas acquisition. Refill sales retain the issued FIFO shell value in a bottle receivable, transferred to empty inventory when physically returned. Excess acknowledged receipts require a known shell basis and recognize a stock gain. Stove products have no shell or LPG-commission component. Package shell costs use the recorded invoice split, the same invoice's package/refill difference, or its verified existing shell basis, captured once at receipt. Unknown splits block receipt until entered.

`monthly_commission_rates` holds one effective Package/Refill amount pair per LPG product/calendar month, with manual/provisional source, calculation evidence, actor, revision and append-only history. All received eligible LPG quantities use actual `received_at` in Bangladesh time. Stove and unrelated products never enter the allocation. Deductions record their actual receipt date and earned month (default previous calendar month). Cumulative deductions minus confirmed manual entitlement are allocated over remaining eligible received quantity × invoice unit price. Inferred rates are explicitly provisional lower bounds; partial deductions never certify the final month's entitlement. Manual rates are never silently overwritten.

Earned monthly commission reduces its historical acquisition batches and establishes supplier receivables. Subsequent consumption flows through FIFO COGS. A later purchase is acquired at its full invoice plus external labour cost; its commission deduction settles the earlier receivable and reduces supplier debt, without reducing that new batch or recognizing revenue again. Pending deductions on unreceived shipments retain a separate clearing liability. Owners can correct received deductions and earned-month assignments with revision guards, recovery keys, reasons and immutable history. Existing real payments remain unchanged.

Normal stock writes consume/add layers incrementally. Backdated movements rebuild the tenant's connected chronological stock history under ordered SKU locks; changed historical monthly rates rebuild affected product histories in the source transaction, append dated journal/cost differences and invalidate all affected monthly savepoints. Ordered item locks precede journal/savepoint locks. Operational approximate buying prices retain automatic suggestions/manual overrides and never enter valuation.

The forward development normalization preserves original journals and source identities. It materializes exact earlier opening valuation evidence and audits the correction of obsolete package sales that deducted an unrelated spare shell. Reconciliation checks the resulting quantities, layer values, stock ledger and financial journals; there is no artificial reporting baseline or invented payment.

## Other obligations and assets

Salary costs accrue once for their contractual month, independently of payment; each contractual month is recognized on its own date, including backdated terms and corrections. Future-month salary payments remain advances until accrual. Bonuses and transit crew costs follow their recorded earning events. Crew, operating and service payments settle obligations or advances without another expense. External transport revenue is independent of collections; purchase transport is settled internally once, capitalized into buyer FIFO and eliminated from consolidated revenue, stock and COGS. Logistics summaries and transit/activity projections read those recognition journals too; vehicle summaries include service expenses with shared costs allocated by their retained transaction percentages.

Loans record lender, principal and receipt/repayment history. Receipt increases cash and principal liability; repayment reduces both. Interest is manually recorded using Spending → Interest, which is always a cost. There is no automatic interest, schedule, compounding or inferred unpaid interest obligation.

`business_assets` records vehicles, equipment and other fixed assets. Book value is cost less explicit, dated depreciation. No automatic depreciation or upward trading-value estimate is applied. Disposal recognizes net proceeds minus remaining book value, retaining the full asset/activity history. Vehicle retirement is permanent and requires active journeys to return; out-of-service vehicles may finish existing journeys but cannot start new ones. Owner authority is enforced in SQL.

Earlier separately posted supplier-credit records remain readable historical evidence. New LPG commission recognition and settlement use the monthly acquisition-rebate model above; the superseded independent commission entry operation is removed.

The shared `finance_obligations` register supports supplemental opening customer/supplier/employee/operating balances, newly incurred unpaid business costs and assets purchased on credit. They can be partially collected or paid from Adjust Balance's recorded balances, using the same wallet/journal path. Existing sales, purchases, salaries and loans retain their original settlement paths. Incurred operating costs recognize an expense once; later settlements do not repeat it. Credit-funded asset payments retain investing cash-flow classification, while unknown opening liabilities remain unclassified. Opening equipment/vehicles use the asset register; inventory uses valued adjustments.

## Coverage, attribution and reporting

The disposable development business reconstructs existing source history instead of absorbing operations into an October 31 opening journal. Sales, purchase recognition/receipts, inventory dispatch/consumption/bottle returns, wallet entries, bonuses, operating events and contractual salary months replay through the same current journal/cost functions without changing operational stock or real payments. Exact cancelling virtual-funding/correction pairs are archived and removed; subsequent wallet balance-after projections are recalculated. Reconciliation assertions reject discrepancies in stock, cash, customer receivables or supplier payables instead of plugging them to profit.

Initial quantities come from current stock less recorded movements. Initial stock values use received invoice evidence; missing shells use Tk 1,500 and missing filled values use Tk 100/kg plus shell. Existing vehicle values retain their labeled capacity-based estimates. These are development assumptions, not verified acquisition costs. Initial net assets are opening capital, with zero assumed retained earnings before available records. Subsequent retained earnings are actual journal revenue less expenses. No arbitrary profit is inserted.

Runtime journals and carrying-cost entries remain immutable. Only the forward development normalization replaces the inspected opening-only projection and establishes chronological evidence. New businesses start from zero, earlier empty reporting periods return zero, and backdated entries extend the earliest-history metadata. There is no date-based suppression of historical profit. Missing essential recorded asset values remain an explicit integrity limitation.

Supplemental opening entries inside a normal selected period are non-profit capital movements in the net-assets reconciliation; they never create profit or operating cash flow. All positions, trends, period values and components use the selected organization or LPG/Stove/Logistics scope. No persisted line uses a shared/unknown business.

Finance → Analysis has one Overview: accumulated and period income, then paired opening/closing assets, liabilities and equity (a compact position selector on phones). Loans and supplier principal appear together as Loans / outstanding dues, while underlying payable accounts, employee obligations, advances and overdrafts retain their classifications. Every summary and total opens the same historical breakdown. `get_accounting_breakdown` returns at most 20 components or movements per page, with search, optional activity start date, selected-component history and separately paged journal lines. Current customer/product/purchase/sale/employee/loan/asset dialogs retain their own permissions and are labeled as current records. Cross-business readers can inspect authorized journal evidence without gaining operational access.

Three charts show financial position, income performance and closing asset composition from the same report. Position trends calculate control-account sign changes using cumulative windows over one period read, correctly handling an advance changing into a payable. Intervals are clipped to the requested dates. Daily charts are bounded to two years; longer periods use month/year intervals. Organization scope always uses the signed-in trusted membership; business-line filters select LPG, Stove, Logistics or their consolidation. PDF exports the loaded Overview, positions, accumulated income, trend values and valuation provenance.

## Savepoints and reconciliation

Private `accounting_snapshots` and `accounting_snapshot_lines` store cumulative month-end account/type/business/entity balances and separate consolidated values. `accounting_balances` selects the nearest valid preceding savepoint independently for each tenant and adds subsequent canonical journal lines. It falls back to full history when none is valid. There is no reporting baseline and no suppressed history. Snapshot revisions, source counts and checksums remain in `accounting_snapshot_audit`.

An INSERT statement trigger on journal lines invalidates the affected month and all following savepoints in the source transaction. It advances the dirty cutoff only when necessary. Source corrections/reversals append normal journals, so the same mechanism covers backdating and retries. Builders lock the tenant's projection state, rebuild at most three months per call and publish all header/line/audit changes atomically. Concurrent writers either precede the build or invalidate its result after waiting. Interrupted builds roll back; the persisted dirty cutoff resumes the next call. Snapshot building runs separately from operational reconciliation to avoid reversed lock order.

`pg_cron` runs private bounded workers. Changed operational records become due at approximately **03:00 Asia/Dhaka**; a minutely worker drains due work. Weekly Friday 03:00 and first-of-month 03:05 sweeps persist composite tenant/entity cursors, enqueue at most 100 records per step and resume after interruption. A global transaction advisory lock prevents duplicate maintenance; each run checks at most 40 records with a 15-second loop budget and a 25-second scheduled statement timeout. Busy operational rows use NOWAIT and retry later. Snapshot workers have their own lock and timeout.

Checks cover customer lifetime/ledger metrics, wallet cash against ledger and journal, inventory quantities/costs/movement coverage, loan principal, payroll accrual/payment allocations, supplier commissions, fixed assets, supplemental obligations, sale/purchase recognition, transit earnings/costs, journal balance and exact snapshot rows. Journal writes already have deferred balance constraints, so only comprehensive sweeps enqueue individual immutable journals. Deterministic customer projections and wallets supported by matching ledger/journal evidence may be repaired; invalid snapshots are rebuilt. Physical stock and original transactions are never silently rewritten. Unresolved differences and runtime errors retain observed/expected data and audit history. Finance's reconciliation disclosure pages tenant-authorized findings.

Customer metrics update atomically with invoices, settlements, cancellation and immutable lines/ledger entries. Unfiltered lifetime sorting reads these projections; date/product scopes retain indexed canonical aggregation. Existing item, inventory-cost, wallet, loan, asset and obligation balances are reused. A tenant/month payroll sync marker prevents repeated historical traversal, invalidates under the payroll lock on new terms and refreshes at month rollover. See [backend performance](backend-performance.md) for workload decisions and measurements.

Raw journal evidence is inside an advanced disclosure. `get_analysis_details` pages headers only, with date/source/account/reference/search filters; opening an entry calls `get_journal_lines` for at most 25 described lines per page. Both RPCs enforce analysis scope independently. Large opening entries never render thousands of rows at once.

## Operational references

`product_buying_price_history` is an independent append-only reference stream. The latest exact-product/type received invoice plus per-unit transport/labour less the active Package/Refill commission pair (latest configured month up to the current month, carried forward until replaced) suggests an approximate price. Manual values survive receipt/commission updates until the owner restores automatic mode. Automatic restoration uses the same live reference as the product summary. Changes retain reasons, dates, purchase basis, commission month/revision and actor; buying and commission histories are paged. There is no dependency from these estimates into journal recognition, inventory pools, COGS, net profit or asset values. Monthly LPG rates are accounting evidence; archived operational commission estimates are retained only as historical evidence.

## Type/category/business reporting

Components group by fixed account, operation type and business. Revenue, expense and equity components aggregate across originating source IDs; those individual transactions remain paged Historical Activity with permission-checked sale, purchase, transit, employee, loan, asset or financial-entry links. Wallet filters use the actual catalog, and a selected business shows that portion's money and running balance. No hardcoded list of obsolete wallet types supplies classification.

Period activity includes revenue, expense, profit/loss, withdrawals, capital, stock acquisitions/change, equipment acquisitions/improvements, receivable/payable changes, loan movements and cash movements. These and the PDF share the report snapshot and selected business scope. Supported opening earnings remain an explicit equity component, distinct from period recognition. See [the operation matrix](financial-type-matrix.md) and [validation](finance-validation.md).

Period stock acquisitions are newly incurred invoice and capitalized internal transport costs less earned acquisition rebates, plus supported owner-contributed/gained stock. Returning an already-owned shell from transit is a stock transfer, not another acquisition. Later supplier deductions settle the earlier rebate receivable without discounting a new batch. Consolidated acquisitions exclude internal transport.
