# Unified accounting and financial analysis

## Accounting boundary

`accounting_journals` and `accounting_lines` are the financial source of truth. Every journal balances signed debits and credits. Account classifications distinguish assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses. Append-only entries retain source, business date, recording time, actor and provenance. A deferred constraint validates each complete journal at commit; direct browser writes are denied.

Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Accumulated profit is the income/expense account balance. Period profit must also equal closing net assets minus opening net assets, minus contributions, plus withdrawals, minus other equity movements. `get_finance_analysis` derives both results from the same journal. Opening entries belong to the opening position and are excluded from period income and cash flow. An authorized reader may refresh deterministic monthly salary accruals; this never authorizes discretionary changes to another business.

`app_private.sync_journal` posts changes to mutable source obligations using a locked source projection. It does not replace old journals. Wallet entries post cash and settle the relevant control account exactly once. `post_wallet_entry` remains the only function updating actual wallet balances. Cash transfers balance through one operation and produce zero business cash flow. Wallet corrections affect equity adjustments, not revenue. Additional capital and owner withdrawals have separate equity accounts.

## Recognition and inventory

Sales recognize revenue and customer receivables independently of collection. Full cancellation reverses the invoice and recorded stock carrying values; actual partial/multi-wallet refunds settle the customer credit separately. A sale without recorded carrying evidence must be reconciled before reversal; the development reconstruction supplies that evidence for historical movements. Sales retains a Cancelled / refunds filter for subsequent settlement.

`inventory_costs` holds separate filled/product and empty-cylinder moving weighted-average pools, reconciled with `items`. Immutable `inventory_cost_entries` record each movement's quantity, value and resulting pool values. Quantities retain the documented LPG stock rules. Stove products use only the filled/product quantity column, with no LPG bottle obligations.

Purchases recognize their external payable and inventory in transit. Receipts transfer actual invoice cost, net invoice discount and allocated labour, into stock. Dispatched empty cylinders remain assets in transit until their filled receipt or cancellation. The final receipt absorbs currency rounding so transit inventory clears exactly. Internal purchase transport allocations are eliminated; actual external transit operating costs are expensed once. Prices and quantities freeze after receipt or any payment. Later supplier rebates are explicit earned credits, not edits to posted receipt costs.

Sales and losses remove recorded weighted-average values, taking the exact remainder when a pool empties. Outstanding returnable cylinders retain a customer bottle asset; later physical receipt transfers that asset to inventory without creating next-period profit. Acknowledged excess receipts recognize a stock gain. Owners add verified opening stock or capital contributions without revenue, or record count gains and losses with their appropriate income/expense consequences. Positive additions require unit costs; reductions use carrying costs. Unvalued stock mutation APIs are removed. Approximate buying prices and commission estimates remain editable operational references, with append-only histories; neither is read by costing or reporting.

Received stove purchases may cancel only when their quantities and original receipt value can be removed from the remaining pools. Original receipt cost is reversed; consumed value is never replaced with an invented price or gain. Actual cash refunds and applied supplier credits retain separate evidence. LPG retains its pre-receipt cancellation boundary and vehicle transit workflow.

## Other obligations and assets

Salary costs accrue once for their contractual month, independently of payment; each contractual month is recognized on its own date, including backdated terms and corrections. Future-month salary payments remain advances until accrual. Bonuses and transit crew costs follow their recorded earning events. Crew, operating and service payments settle obligations or advances without another expense. External transport revenue is independent of collections; purchase transport is internal and eliminated. Logistics summaries and transit/activity projections read those recognition journals too; vehicle summaries include service expenses while shared business costs remain unallocated.

Loans record lender, principal and receipt/repayment history. Receipt increases cash and principal liability; repayment reduces both. Interest is manually recorded using Spending → Interest, which is always a cost. There is no automatic interest, schedule, compounding or inferred unpaid interest obligation.

`business_assets` records vehicles, equipment and other fixed assets. Book value is cost less explicit, dated depreciation. No automatic depreciation or upward trading-value estimate is applied. Disposal recognizes net proceeds minus remaining book value, retaining the full asset/activity history. Vehicle retirement is permanent and requires active journeys to return; out-of-service vehicles may finish existing journeys but cannot start new ones. Owner authority is enforced in SQL.

Earned supplier commissions recognize a supplier receivable and income on their earned date. Applying a credit to a matching supplier/category purchase settles payable and receivable, without more income or cash. Cancellation restores applied credits. Invoice discounts remain reductions in inventory acquisition cost and are not also commission income.

The shared `finance_obligations` register supports supplemental opening customer/supplier/employee/operating balances, newly incurred unpaid business costs and assets purchased on credit. They can be partially collected or paid from Finance's Opening balances register, using the same wallet/journal path. Existing sales, purchases, salaries and loans retain their original settlement paths. Incurred operating costs recognize an expense once; later settlements do not repeat it. Credit-funded asset payments retain investing cash-flow classification, while unknown opening liabilities remain unclassified. Opening equipment/vehicles use the asset register; inventory uses valued adjustments.

## Coverage, attribution and reporting

The disposable development business reconstructs existing source history instead of absorbing operations into an October 31 opening journal. Sales, purchase recognition/receipts, inventory dispatch/consumption/bottle returns, wallet entries, bonuses, operating events and contractual salary months replay through the same current journal/cost functions without changing operational stock or real payments. Exact cancelling virtual-funding/correction pairs are archived and removed; subsequent wallet balance-after projections are recalculated. Reconciliation assertions reject discrepancies in stock, cash, customer receivables or supplier payables instead of plugging them to profit.

Initial quantities come from current stock less recorded movements. Initial stock values use received invoice evidence; missing shells use Tk 1,500 and missing filled values use Tk 100/kg plus shell. Existing vehicle values retain their labeled capacity-based estimates. These are development assumptions, not verified acquisition costs. Initial net assets are opening capital, with zero assumed retained earnings before available records. Subsequent retained earnings are actual journal revenue less expenses. No arbitrary profit is inserted.

Runtime journals and carrying-cost entries remain immutable. Only the forward development normalization replaces the inspected opening-only projection and establishes chronological evidence. New businesses start from zero, earlier empty reporting periods return zero, and backdated entries extend the earliest-history metadata. There is no date-based suppression of historical profit. Missing essential recorded asset values remain an explicit integrity limitation.

Opening entries on the first reporting day belong to its opening position. Supplemental opening entries dated later in a selected period are equity adjustments in the net-assets profit reconciliation; neither creates profit or operating cash flow. Financial positions remain combined under activity filters; income, expenses and cash retain LPG/stove/logistics/shared attribution. Shared costs are never allocated arbitrarily.

Finance offers compact overview metrics and wallet balances, revenue/expense/profit and cash trends, expense distribution, opening/closing assets-versus-liabilities charts, net-worth trends, activity comparisons and readable financial breakdowns. All use `get_finance_analysis`. Positions aggregate control balances on the server, retaining advances/overdraft classification and named cash wallets without downloading every customer or purchase. Daily trends are bounded to two years; longer periods use month/year intervals. Authorized business scope uses trusted membership and explicit `business_analysis_access` grants on every request. PDF exports the loaded period, figures, trend values and estimate provenance.

Raw journal evidence is inside an advanced disclosure. `get_analysis_details` pages headers only, with date/source/account/reference/search filters; opening an entry calls `get_journal_lines` for at most 25 described lines per page. Both RPCs enforce analysis scope independently. Large opening entries never render thousands of rows at once.

## Operational references

`product_buying_price_history` is an independent append-only reference stream. The latest exact-product/type received invoice plus per-unit transport/labour less configured product commission suggests an approximate price. Manual values survive receipt/commission updates until the owner restores automatic mode. Changes retain reasons, dates, purchase basis and actor; buying and commission histories are paged. There is no dependency from these estimates into journal recognition, inventory pools, COGS, net profit or asset values. Actual earned supplier credits remain a separate receivable/income/settlement workflow.

## Verification

It removes only its disposable database. Historical suites still execute at the preceding model's replay boundary in `run-purchases.mjs`.

`node tests/unified-accounting.browser.mjs` uses intercepted APIs for layout/interaction checks. `node supabase/tests/unified-accounting-api.mjs --vps --browser` verifies the active VPS/Worker and burner membership, then uses normal Auth/PostgREST and published browser flows on explicitly created disposable businesses. It cleans only those fixtures and compares original-business fingerprints. These checks do not establish physical printing or external bank/mobile-provider settlement.

`node tests/sitewide-contrast.mjs --vps` reviews major pages, settings and primary dialogs in desktop/mobile light and dark themes. `node tests/workflow-visual-review.mjs --vps` inspects saved records, nested pricing/payment/vehicle dialogs and image fallbacks without submitting mutations. Both measure the actual workspace scroll container as well as document width; hidden horizontal clipping must not pass as a responsive layout.
