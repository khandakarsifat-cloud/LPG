# Unified accounting and financial analysis

## Accounting boundary

`accounting_journals` and `accounting_lines` are the financial source of truth. Every journal balances signed debits and credits independently for each business. New financial actions share one journal per action/date; employee/month salary recognition retains its own date. Account classifications distinguish assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses. Append-only entries retain source, business date, recording time, actor and provenance. A deferred constraint validates each complete journal at commit; direct browser writes are denied.

Assets = Liabilities + Capital + Cumulative Revenue − Cumulative Expenses − Cumulative Owner Withdrawals, plus separately identified legitimate other equity movements. Accumulated income is cumulative revenue less cumulative expenses; it is already part of equity, never another independent equity account. Period income is closing accumulated income less opening accumulated income. It also equals the change in net assets after contributions, withdrawals and other equity movements. `get_finance_analysis` establishes contractual accruals, then reads all positions, totals and trends through one stable MVCC snapshot so concurrent backdated writes cannot mix financial states.

All monthly, yearly and custom positions use cumulative business-local cutoffs. A normal opening position ends immediately before the first selected day; closing includes the last selected day. Same-day supplemental opening evidence changes capital during that period without creating profit or operating cash flow. All-time uses the original opening entries at the earliest supported business date, then all subsequent history. Initial liabilities reduce opening capital. Authorized reporting may establish deterministic contractual salary accruals; it never authorizes discretionary activity in another business.

`app_private.sync_journal` computes changes to mutable source obligations using a locked source projection; a transaction-local collector posts the final summarized action. It does not replace old journals. Wallet entries post cash and settle the relevant control account exactly once. `post_wallet_entry` remains the only function updating actual wallet balances. Cash transfers balance through one operation and produce zero business cash flow. Wallet corrections affect equity adjustments, not revenue. Additional capital and owner withdrawals have separate equity accounts.

## Recognition and inventory

Sales recognize revenue and customer receivables independently of collection. Full cancellation reverses the invoice and recorded stock carrying values; actual partial/multi-wallet refunds settle the customer credit separately. A sale without recorded carrying evidence must be reconciled before reversal; the development reconstruction supplies that evidence for historical movements. Sales retains a Cancelled / refunds filter for subsequent settlement.

`fifo_component_layers` records each LPG Gas and Bottle acquisition with its original quantity, date, external value and internal transport. `fifo_positions` tracks availability and exact remaining cost by physical batch. A filled position links its Gas acquisition and Bottle acquisition, preserving receipt order and the rounding of each historical slice. `fifo_consumptions` records original issues and their reversals. The private `fifo_layers` view projects the filled and empty positions for existing reports, and `inventory_costs` holds the current stock valuation. Immutable `inventory_cost_entries` and journals preserve original postings and dated corrections. The mutable movement-cost cache lets chronological replay calculate the exact correction against the previous result.

Filled LPG units contain one bottle plus gas. Package issues consume both historical components. At dispatch, `fifo_purchase_holdings` records the exact available empty slices moved into supplier custody. Receipt closes those holdings and links the original Bottle acquisitions to the new Gas. Refill issues retain the outgoing historical shell basis as a replacement claim until physically exchanged. Actual incoming cylinders use the received brand’s current Bottle buying price captured on receipt. The difference from the outgoing basis is a signed bottle-exchange gain or loss, separately traceable from gas FIFO COGS. That incoming snapshot becomes its future FIFO acquisition basis. Excess acknowledged receipts use the same current-reference rule. Missing Bottle references block receipt.

Monthly/product commission calculations and rates remain removed. Manual purchase rebates appear as Company rebate / Other Income with the purchasing business, reducing supplier dues while preserving gross acquisition costs. Released company credit is a supplier receivable; later application is not new revenue. Gas and Bottle acquisition costs come from separately priced purchase components, attributable labour and internal transport; no rebate assumption changes stock or COGS.



Normal stock writes maintain FIFO incrementally. Backdated movements rebuild connected chronological stock history under ordered SKU locks, append dated cost/journal differences and invalidate affected month-end savepoints. Original incoming exchange snapshots remain immutable. Cancellation claim/exchange reversals also refresh after historical revaluation. Operational references never rewrite historical valuation.

The forward development normalization preserves original journals and source identities. It materializes exact earlier opening valuation evidence and audits the correction of obsolete package sales that deducted an unrelated spare shell. Reconciliation checks the resulting quantities, layer values, stock ledger and financial journals; there is no artificial reporting baseline or invented payment.

## Other obligations and assets

Salary costs accrue once for their contractual month, independently of payment; each contractual month is recognized on its own date, including backdated terms and corrections. Future-month salary payments remain advances until accrual. Bonuses and transit crew costs follow their recorded earning events. Crew, operating and service payments settle obligations or advances without another expense. External transport revenue is independent of collections; purchase transport is allocated without cash once, capitalized into buyer FIFO and retained in additive Combined revenue, Supplies and COGS. Logistics summaries and transit/activity projections read the maintained recognition targets and immutable internal settlement evidence; vehicle summaries include service expenses with shared costs allocated by their retained transaction percentages.

Loans record counterparty, borrowed/lent direction, principal and receipt/repayment history. Money lent is a separate `loan_receivable` asset: lending reduces wallet cash and collection restores it without profit. Existing borrowing remains `loan_payable`. Collection locks the source loan, rejects overcollection or dates before its history, inherits the original business allocation and uses the same retained-request contract. Receipt increases cash and principal liability; repayment reduces both. Interest is manually recorded using Spending → Interest, which is always a cost. There is no automatic interest, schedule, compounding or inferred unpaid interest obligation.

`business_assets` records vehicles, equipment and other fixed assets. Book value is cost less explicit, dated depreciation. No automatic depreciation or upward trading-value estimate is applied. Disposal recognizes net proceeds minus remaining book value, retaining the full asset/activity history. Vehicle retirement is permanent and requires active journeys to return; out-of-service vehicles may finish existing journeys but cannot start new ones. Owner authority is enforced in SQL.



The shared `finance_obligations` register supports supplemental opening customer/supplier/employee/operating balances, newly incurred unpaid business costs and assets purchased on credit. They can be partially collected or paid from Adjust Balance's recorded balances, using the same wallet/journal path. Existing sales, purchases, salaries and loans retain their original settlement paths. Incurred operating costs recognize an expense once; later settlements do not repeat it. Credit-funded asset payments retain investing cash-flow classification, while unknown opening liabilities remain unclassified. Opening equipment/vehicles use the asset register; inventory uses valued adjustments.

## Coverage, attribution and reporting

The current component architecture is established on a clean development baseline after a verified, recoverable full backup. Old development representations are removed explicitly; there is no runtime compatibility fallback or fabricated historical attribution.

Fresh businesses start at zero. Opening inventory requires explicit Gas and Bottle carrying values, and owner contributions use Capital. No fixed guessed shell cost or kg-based estimate is supplied. Equipment, liabilities and loans use their existing identifiable source workflows.

Runtime journals and carrying-cost entries remain immutable. Only the forward development normalization replaces the inspected opening-only projection and establishes chronological evidence. New businesses start from zero, earlier empty reporting periods return zero, and backdated entries extend the earliest-history metadata. There is no date-based suppression of historical profit. Missing essential recorded asset values remain an explicit integrity limitation.

Supplemental opening entries inside a normal selected period are non-profit capital movements in the net-assets reconciliation; they never create profit or operating cash flow. All positions, trends, period values and components use the selected organization or LPG/Stove/Logistics scope. No persisted line uses a shared/unknown business.

Finance → Analysis has one Overview: accumulated and period income, then paired opening/closing assets, liabilities and equity (a compact position selector on phones). Loans and supplier principal appear together as Loans / outstanding dues, while underlying payable accounts, employee obligations, advances and overdrafts retain their classifications. Every summary and total opens the same historical breakdown. `get_accounting_breakdown` returns at most 20 components or movements per page, with search, optional activity start date, selected-component history and separately paged journal lines. Current customer/product/purchase/sale/employee/loan/asset dialogs retain their own permissions and are labeled as current records. Cross-business readers can inspect authorized journal evidence without gaining operational access.

Three charts show financial position, income performance and closing asset composition from the same report. Position trends calculate control-account sign changes using cumulative windows over one period read, correctly handling an advance changing into a payable. Intervals are clipped to the requested dates. Daily charts are bounded to two years; longer periods use month/year intervals. Organization scope always uses the signed-in trusted membership; business-line filters select LPG, Stove, Logistics or their additive Combined total. PDF exports the loaded Overview, positions, accumulated income, trend values and valuation provenance.

## Savepoints and reconciliation

Private `accounting_snapshots` and `accounting_snapshot_lines` store cumulative month-end account/type/business/entity balances and separate consolidated values. `accounting_balances` selects the nearest valid preceding savepoint independently for each tenant and adds subsequent canonical journal lines. It falls back to full history when none is valid. There is no reporting baseline and no suppressed history. Snapshot revisions, source counts and checksums remain in `accounting_snapshot_audit`.

An INSERT statement trigger on journal lines invalidates the affected month and all following savepoints in the source transaction. It advances the dirty cutoff only when necessary. Source corrections/reversals append normal journals, so the same mechanism covers backdating and retries. Builders lock the tenant's projection state, rebuild at most three months per call and publish all header/line/audit changes atomically. Concurrent writers either precede the build or invalidate its result after waiting. Interrupted builds roll back; the persisted dirty cutoff resumes the next call. Snapshot building runs separately from operational reconciliation to avoid reversed lock order.

`pg_cron` runs private bounded workers. Changed operational records become due at approximately **03:00 Asia/Dhaka**; a minutely worker drains due work. Weekly Friday 03:00 and first-of-month 03:05 sweeps persist composite tenant/entity cursors, enqueue at most 100 records per step and resume after interruption. A global transaction advisory lock prevents duplicate maintenance; each run checks at most 40 records with a 15-second loop budget and a 25-second scheduled statement timeout. Busy operational rows use NOWAIT and retry later. Snapshot workers have their own lock and timeout.

Checks cover customer lifetime/ledger metrics, wallet cash against ledger and journal, inventory quantities/costs/movement coverage, loan principal, payroll accrual/payment allocations, fixed assets, supplemental obligations, sale/purchase recognition, transit earnings/costs, journal balance and exact snapshot rows. Journal writes already have deferred balance constraints, so only comprehensive sweeps enqueue individual immutable journals. Deterministic customer projections and wallets supported by matching ledger/journal evidence may be repaired; invalid snapshots are rebuilt. Physical stock and original transactions are never silently rewritten. Unresolved differences and runtime errors retain observed/expected data and audit history. Finance's reconciliation disclosure pages tenant-authorized findings.

Customer metrics update atomically with invoices, settlements, cancellation and immutable lines/ledger entries. Unfiltered lifetime sorting reads these projections; date/product scopes retain indexed canonical aggregation. Existing item, inventory-cost, wallet, loan, asset and obligation balances are reused. A tenant/month payroll sync marker prevents repeated historical traversal, invalidates under the payroll lock on new terms and refreshes at month rollover. See [backend performance](backend-performance.md) for workload decisions and measurements.

Raw journal evidence is inside an advanced disclosure. `get_analysis_details` pages headers only, with date/source/account/reference/search filters; opening an entry calls `get_journal_lines` for at most 25 described lines per page. Both RPCs enforce analysis scope independently. Large opening entries never render thousands of rows at once.

## Operational references

`product_buying_price_history` is an append-only Gas/Bottle reference stream with reasons, actor, source purchase and dates. Owners may edit either component. A newer purchase receipt updates acquired components to actual invoice price plus value-allocated labour/transport, replacing earlier manual references; Gas-only receipt leaves Bottle unchanged. Backdated receipts cannot replace a newer reference. Frontend Refill shows Gas, Empty shows Bottle and Package shows their sum. Reference edits do not alter earlier inventory/COGS or physical receipt snapshots.

## Type/category/business reporting

Components group by fixed account, type and business. Individual transactions remain paged source-linked activity. Wallet filters use the real catalog; business filters show attributed movements while the displayed wallet balance remains the real wallet balance.

Period activity includes revenue, expense, profit/loss, withdrawals, capital, stock acquisitions/change, equipment acquisitions/improvements, receivable/payable changes, loan movements and cash movements. These and the PDF share the report snapshot and selected business scope. Supported opening earnings remain an explicit equity component, distinct from period recognition. See [the operation matrix](financial-type-matrix.md) and [validation](finance-validation.md).

Period stock acquisitions are newly incurred component invoice costs and capitalized internal transport plus supported owner-contributed stock. Returning an owned shell from custody transfers an existing asset. Combined acquisitions retain the purchasing business’s capitalized transport.

Combined is an additive management view of LPG, Stove and Logistics. It sums each business’s independently classified opening/closing position and retains all transport revenue and capitalized transport cost. Transport enters the buyer’s Supplies and becomes COGS only on related issues; it is never also posted as an operating expense. Combined can therefore include Logistics profit while the buyer’s matching cost remains unsold inventory. Reports, trends, activity and PDF exports use ordinary journal amounts. Original external-cost columns, FIFO evidence and savepoints remain unchanged for historical audit/reconciliation; they do not drive Combined reporting.
