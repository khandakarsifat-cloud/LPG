# Finance and wallet accounting

## Canonical dimensions

Every financial line carries a valid Type, fixed account Category and LPG, Stove or Logistics Business. [The complete operation/category matrix](financial-type-matrix.md) is the contract. `financial_types` contains immutable system definitions and tenant-scoped editable custom definitions. Custom types select an allowed account and percentages totaling 100; immutable `financial_entries` retain their category, name, revision, exact business cents and allocations. Settings edits/deactivation affect future entries. The obsolete spending-category table/RPCs and empty standalone commission model are removed.

Wallets retain signed `business_balances` whose sum must equal their total. Every immutable wallet movement stores its allocation, exact business amounts and resulting balances. The single posting trigger updates all portions under the existing wallet lock. Source-derived payroll, loans, obligations, assets, purchases and sales determine attribution automatically; employee salary months/advances retain their original allocation. Negative business and total balances are allowed. Transfers specify both wallet and business at each end, including the same wallet. Business equity is reallocated; no revenue, expense or inter-business receivable/debt is created.

Internal purchase transport is paid once at dispatch from the selected purchasing wallet/business into Logistics Cash Register. Cancellation of an unreceived shipment returns the same business amounts. Transit completion makes no further charge. Buyer transport overhead follows the original filled-unit FIFO acquisition/consumption links, separately from the recorded invoice shell basis. Internal revenue and transport carrying costs have zero consolidated value, including the internal amount retained in unsold stock and subsequent COGS/returns. External invoice costs, received-month commissions and physical shell accounting retain their original rules.

Custom equipment creates one asset with allocated ownership. Later depreciation, qualifying improvements, credit liabilities, refunds and disposal use that same asset. Vehicles always belong to Logistics. Supported opening accumulated earnings reclassify opening equity without cash, revenue or period profit.

## Ownership and migration

The balanced accounting journal is the financial source of truth. `wallet_transactions` remains the sole cash-movement ledger, and `wallets` projects its balances; each cash movement posts to the journal atomically. Every tenant receives Cash Register, bKash, Nagad and Primary Bank Account. The virtual Logistics wallet is removed. Transit revenue and incurred costs live independently of wallet balances. The internal `dealership` key identifies Primary Bank Account; its UUID, signed balance and historical relationships are preserved in place. No historical POS payments or income are invented.

`opening_balance = balance − sum(historical wallet entries)` captures incomplete historic cash-ledger coverage at migration time. Historical entries receive a balance-after value from that baseline and their original chronological order. A wallet reconciles as `balance = opening_balance + sum(entries)` after transition. The journal reconstructs dated historical entries and explicitly recorded opening balances. Development normalization archives/removes only exactly cancelling obsolete virtual-funding pairs; real balances and payments remain reconciled. Owners record additional opening funds through Finance's opening-cash operation, paired with opening equity; ordinary balance corrections use other equity adjustments. Employee payouts and returned funds use the same posting trigger; monthly salary records and revisions move no cash.

Default wallets stay active and cannot be deleted through the API. Finance managers may rename wallets and edit details; method/category and system identity remain fixed. Custom wallets may be archived only at zero balance. Archived wallets retain their ledger but are excluded from payment selectors. Wallet edits use revisions and audit records; payment records preserve the wallet name and method captured when paid. Wallet ledger entries also carry name/method snapshots, including purchase funding and refunds; earlier labels are snapshotted at transition when original labels are unavailable.

## One posting model

`app_private.post_wallet_entry` applies every new wallet entry to its tenant-qualified wallet under a row lock and stores the actual balance afterward. POS, collections, adjustments, transfers, purchases and logistics insert ledger evidence; they do not separately update balances. Historical money and stock are never recalculated from incomplete evidence.

`sale_payments` records received amounts, wallet, method, checkout/collection phase, reference and actor. Checkout inserts one negative customer invoice charge. Each received payment inserts one payment record, one wallet credit and one positive customer receipt, linked by payment ID. `sales.paid_amount` is the current settlement; `paid_at_checkout` preserves the initial paid amount. Checkout payment attribution is read from immutable `sale_payments`; it is not duplicated as a JSON column on `sales`. An invoice's outstanding amount is `total_amount + additional_charges - paid_amount`; customer outstanding is the negative customer-ledger sum. Unreceived amounts create no wallet entry.

Default checkout sends null/omits `p_payments` for full Cash Register payment. An explicit array supports at most 20 positive finite entries with at most two decimals; an empty array records no initial payment. A zero-total sale creates no payment. Collections target one completed sale and cannot exceed its remaining balance. Previously paid amounts without recorded methods remain paid. `get_sale_settlement.unattributed_paid` is the paid header minus attributed payment rows, and receipts show that provenance explicitly. No version flag changes settlement behavior or permits bypassing receipt validation.

Transfers append equal opposite entries with one operation ID and counterparty links. Transfers and operational settlements allow signed deficits; no available-funds gate creates a second balance policy. Adjustments append a signed entry and mandatory reason. Correct a manual balance entry by recording an opposite adjustment referencing it. Purchase payments and refunds use `purchase_payments`, immutable source-backed obligations and the same Finance request/ledger architecture. Cancellation is OWNER-only and operational; wallet refunds and transport-funding reversals are settled separately, while truck operating expenses remain intact. See the purchase lifecycle guide. Owners can cancel a sale with recorded post-opening carrying evidence and record staged partial/multi-wallet customer refunds. Original payments are never deleted; the journal and customer ledger retain compensating evidence.

## Atomicity, authorization and recovery

Checkout preserves server price validation, ordered stock locks, tenant/request serialization, stored request payload/results, and atomic rollback. Payments participate in that same database transaction. A retried key returns its original result and rejects changed payment details. The canonical migration fills missing checkout-payload keys once; saved request IDs/results remain recoverable.

Collection locks the sale before wallets, preventing competing requests from overpaying. Multi-wallet operations lock participating wallets in UUID order, consistent with purchases. `finance_requests` serializes finance request IDs and saves the original payload/result. Client session storage retains uncertain writes per tenant/user/tab and exposes recovery before another transaction. A lost HTTP response never requires a new payment key. Wallet ledger/customer payment history is append-only; explicit browser writes remain denied.

RLS uses trusted active membership and tenant-qualified foreign keys. `finance:view` or `finance:manage` permits wallet/ledger reads; `finance:manage` gates wallet editing, transfers, adjustments and collections. POS can read payment destinations through `get_payment_wallets`, which exposes names/methods without balances or account details. Existing sales/receipt permissions permit reading persisted sale payments. Purchase managers can read eligible destination names without Finance balances, and purchase view/manage permissions gate the bounded purchase settlement API. Customer permissions expose an aggregate customer balance without opening the financial ledger.

## Frontend and query boundaries

[Finance](../../src/pages/Finance.tsx), [useFinance](../../src/hooks/useFinance.ts), shared payment entries and saved-sale payment panels use TanStack Query invalidation across Finance, wallets, customers and sales. Purchase/logistics mutations also invalidate Finance. The topbar displays combined wallet funds, from real accounts.

The ledger filters on the server before sorting/pagination, using 25-row pages (RPC maximum 100), tenant/wallet/date/type/amount indexes and narrow matching rows before fetching page details. A `pg_trgm` GIN index searches an immutable document captured with each entry (description, wallet label, payment reference and customer identity). Search follows the project's space-AND/comma-OR convention; token parsing happens once, literals are quoted/escaped, and tenant/filter parameters stay bound. Very short search terms and count/amount aggregates can still scan matching history. Inclusive date filters use `Asia/Dhaka`; minimum/maximum compare absolute entry amounts. Running balances are stored per wallet and do not change when filters/sorts change. Received/paid-out totals describe matching money movements, including both sides of transfers; they are not revenue/profit reports. No full ledger is downloaded by the browser.

Customer dues are server-paged. Saved payment history is ten rows per page; receipts aggregate persisted payments by wallet snapshot/method/phase and identify later collections. Reprints show current persisted settlement and never rerun checkout. Both receipt widths use the same content formatter as previews; 58mm uses stacked product lines, 80mm uses amount-aligned product headings. USB paper width is explicit in its printer configuration because Windows queue names are not reliable width detection. Browser width is remembered per tenant/browser; choose matching media in the OS print dialog.

## Employee payments

Employee salary, named bonus and linked driver/helper payments append immutable `employee_payment_entries` and exactly one wallet debit per entry through the existing posting trigger. Salary months and revisions remain distinct from actual wallet movements. Bonuses have custom names and never change salary balances. Actual returned funds append linked payment returns and credits to the original wallets. Finance retains salary, bonus, trip payment and return classifications; removed payment categories cannot be created. Ledger details link to the employee profile under employee/payroll permission.

Linked driver/helper payments use any active real wallet with payroll and Logistics management permissions. All outstanding crew and operating costs must be settled before completion; completion makes no wallet posting. Historical employee payments create no invented wallet entries. See [Employees](employees-and-payroll.md) for salary-month rules, recovery, corrections and historical evidence.

## Additional settlement costs and historical expenses

`20261026035440_dynamic_settlement_receipts.sql` reuses the original later exchange-charge mechanism: a collection header's immutable `fee` records Additional Settlement Cost, `sales.additional_charges` accumulates it, and one negative `customer_transactions.invoice_charge` creates the customer liability. Original `total_amount`, Exchange Cost (`exchange_fee`) and checkout payment snapshots remain unchanged. Recording a cost creates no business expense, wallet debit or payment. Collect payment receives it through the existing partial/multi-wallet workflow; only money actually received credits wallets.

Bottle collection requires `sales:collect_bottles`; a positive cost additionally requires `finance:manage`. Receipt, actual-product stock, customer charge and retained Finance request commit atomically. Tenant/request locks and the sale row lock serialize retries, payments and collections. Changed request payloads are rejected. Completed historical expense/fee requests replay their original result before new validation. Unsupported expense fields are rejected; they are never silently converted into customer charges. Historical `sale_bottle_expense` entries, wallet snapshots and prior customer fees remain audited and unchanged.

Physical over-receipts are saved without capping, with acknowledgement for unexpected or cumulative above-product quantities. Each sale's bottle obligation clamps at zero before aggregation, so excess bottles never cancel another sale's debt. Further collection is disabled once that sale is settled; original uncertain requests remain recoverable. Money and bottle obligations settle independently.

`get_sale_receipt` reads current identities, original transaction values, current charges/payments and all received bottles in one statement snapshot. Preview refreshes on every open and supports manual refresh; print reloads through the same builder. Receipts show Exchange Cost and Additional Settlement Cost separately, all actual received products, current total payable/paid/due and Bottle Settled independently of payment status. Payment records retain their original method/wallet snapshots.

## Configured financial entries

Finance and Dashboard use the same reviewed financial-entry form. Select a custom type, wallet, amount, date and description. The type supplies the fixed category and business percentages; an optional disclosure permits a transaction override. Revenue/capital credit cash; expenses/withdrawals debit it; equipment debits an identifiable asset instead of profit. Stock, loans, customer/supplier settlement and payroll retain their dedicated source workflows.

Settings → Financial types manages defaults, names and activation with revision guards and definition history. Wallet/source details expose actual returned-fund entry, with source locking, bounded partial returns and recovery keys. Returns retain original category/ownership even after type edits or deactivation. Cumulative cent boundaries ensure several partial returns restore exactly the original business amounts.


## Analysis and management

Finance has Analysis, Wallet Ledger, Financial Entries and Loans navigation. Adjust Balance integrates opening funds, capital investments, corrections, recorded obligations and fixed assets. Duplicate activity, spending-history, customer-dues and capital interfaces are removed; source records and accounting remain available through their canonical ledger or management operation. The [unified accounting guide](financial-analysis.md) owns the current recognition and analysis contracts. Every wallet entry posts a balanced journal, settling the appropriate receivable, liability, advance or equity account. Manual costs and Interest reduce profit; owner withdrawals and principal do not. Transfers preserve consolidated cash and create no revenue or expense. Wallet corrections use equity adjustments.

Analysis aggregates the journal for opening/closing financial positions, P&L, capital, cash flow, trends and audit drill-downs. Inventory carrying values come from the same movement-backed cost pools used by Inventory. Future salary payments are advances until monthly accrual. Earned supplier credits remain receivables until applied to a matching supplier purchase; application is not additional income. Supplementary opening balances, incurred business costs and credit-funded assets settle through one `finance_obligations` register without another expense.

Loans and asset cost/depreciation/disposal are owner-only. Monthly LPG commissions are managed in the product modal; received deductions in purchase details. Interest remains a manually paid custom expense type, without automatic interest, schedules or inferred unpaid interest. Cash movements retain their actual accounting date independently of recording time. Reports explicitly identify development estimates, the accounting start and incomplete coverage; unsupported historic profit or capital attribution is never filled with zero.

Every report uses the signed-in trusted tenant. The obsolete multi-organization selector and cross-organization grant model are removed. See the accounting guide for disposable migration/reconciliation/concurrency, intercepted browser and real Auth/published-browser acceptance commands.

Custom type categories are exactly Withdrawal, Expense, Capital, Revenue and Equipment. Internal journal accounts retain the detail needed for assets, liabilities and double-entry posting; they are not extra category choices. Financial Entries lists original custom-entry snapshots and returned funds with category, business, date and source filters. Wallet Ledger resolves payroll sources using distinct SQL variable/alias names, accepts the actual empty-string browser filters, applies AND/comma-OR token search and sorts selected business amounts consistently.
