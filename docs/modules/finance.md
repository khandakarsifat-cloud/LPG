# Finance and wallet accounting

## Canonical dimensions

Every financial line carries a valid Type, fixed account Category and LPG, Stove or Logistics Business. [The complete operation/category matrix](financial-type-matrix.md) is the contract. `financial_types` contains immutable system definitions and tenant-scoped editable custom definitions. Custom types select an allowed account and percentages totaling 100; immutable `financial_entries` retain their category, name, revision, exact business cents and allocations. Settings edits/deactivation affect future entries. The obsolete spending-category table/RPCs are removed.

Each real wallet has one signed balance. Immutable operating movements retain their source and exact LPG/Stove/Logistics allocation, but no wallet stores business sub-balances. Business Cash is pooled across all real wallets and may be negative. Transfers move principal between two different real wallets with exactly two opposite ledger entries; they have no business allocation, equity, profit or debt effect.

Internal purchase transport records a noncash allocation between the purchasing business and Logistics at dispatch. No wallet is selected or moved. Buyer FIFO retains the allocated transport cost; Logistics recognizes its internal revenue. Combined retains that revenue and the matching buyer cost in transit, Supplies and subsequent COGS; it is the sum of the separate business figures. Cancelling an unreceived delivery reverses the allocation; received Stove returns expense performed delivery without inventing a transit asset.

Custom equipment creates one asset with allocated ownership. Later depreciation, qualifying improvements, credit liabilities, refunds and disposal use that same asset. Vehicles always belong to Logistics. Supported opening accumulated earnings reclassify opening equity without cash, revenue or period profit.

## Ownership and migration

The balanced accounting journal is the financial source of truth. `wallet_transactions` remains the sole cash-movement ledger, and `wallets` projects its balances; each cash movement posts to the journal atomically. Every tenant receives Cash Register, bKash, Nagad and Primary Bank Account. The virtual Logistics wallet is removed. Transit revenue and incurred costs live independently of wallet balances. The internal `dealership` key identifies Primary Bank Account; its UUID, signed balance and historical relationships are preserved in place. No historical POS payments or income are invented.

`opening_balance = balance − sum(historical wallet entries)` captures incomplete historic cash-ledger coverage at migration time. Historical entries receive a balance-after value from that baseline and their original chronological order. A wallet reconciles as `balance = opening_balance + sum(entries)` after transition. The journal reconstructs dated historical entries and explicitly recorded opening balances. Development normalization archives/removes only exactly cancelling obsolete virtual-funding pairs; real balances and payments remain reconciled. Owners record additional opening funds through Finance's opening-cash operation, paired with opening equity; ordinary balance corrections use other equity adjustments. Employee payouts and returned funds use the same posting trigger; monthly salary records and revisions move no cash.

Default wallets stay active and cannot be deleted through the API. Finance managers may rename wallets and edit details; method/category and system identity remain fixed. Custom wallets may be archived only at zero balance. Archived wallets retain their ledger but are excluded from payment selectors. Wallet edits use revisions and audit records; payment records preserve the wallet name and method captured when paid. Wallet ledger entries also carry name/method snapshots, including purchase funding and refunds; earlier labels are snapshotted at transition when original labels are unavailable.

## Summarized financial actions

Frontend ownership: [AccountingExplorer](../../src/components/finance/AccountingExplorer.tsx) owns the activity browser and advanced filters; [AccountingBreakdown](../../src/components/finance/AccountingBreakdown.tsx) supplies metric drilldowns. Both reuse [AccountingEntryCard](../../src/components/finance/AccountingEntryCard.tsx), [accountingEntry](../../src/lib/accountingEntry.ts) for action wording/effects, and [JournalAudit](../../src/components/finance/JournalAudit.tsx) for paged actual lines. [useAccounting](../../src/hooks/useAccounting.ts) owns RPC reads; [accounting-activity.css](../../src/components/finance/accounting-activity.css) owns compact responsive cards and phone Debit/Credit layout.

Public financial commands and the payroll scheduler open a transaction-local accounting collector. Existing source triggers, source locks, request identities and recovery results remain canonical; derived journal identities preserve the independent POS/Finance command namespaces. The collector finalizes one journal per action/business date, retaining separate employee/month salary recognition. It combines equivalent lines by account, business, meaningful type and necessary wallet/counterparty/asset/custody identity, removes zero-net clearing effects and posts once. Deferred guards reject unfinished actions. Private implementation functions preserve the original authorization and ownership; there is no legacy posting path or durable duplicate event store.

Checkout combines invoice, real payment legs, FIFO issues and actual bottle effects. Historical transport joins the same stock/COGS line; its external-cost value remains private reconciliation evidence. Receipts net transit reclassifications; immediate bonuses, Logistics collections/settlements and asset purchases net their clearing accounts. Later payments, returns, corrections and refunds remain distinct dated actions. Nonfinancial changes and real-wallet transfers create no financial journal. Historical journals remain unchanged.

Product detail stays in sales/purchases, inventory cost entries, FIFO layers/consumptions and actual bottle receipts. Source replay targets omit redundant empty/null fields and repeated equal amounts on future writes. Product reconciliation compares those sources with FIFO, then checks both external and full carrying values against aggregate inventory control accounts. Trip views read their maintained recognition targets and transport settlement evidence; canonical financial positions still read journals. Cash-flow categories use immutable wallet legs so header summarization cannot hide collections or payments.

Principal repayments retain original per-business principal cents. Proposed payment allocations are capped to still-outstanding ownership; remaining cents go deterministically to businesses with principal left. This prevents fractional repayments over-settling a business or leaving cents at full settlement. Inconsistent historical allocations block another repayment for explicit source reconciliation; no historical wallet entry is rewritten.

## One posting model

`app_private.post_wallet_entry` applies every new wallet entry to its tenant-qualified wallet under a row lock and stores the actual balance afterward. POS, collections, adjustments, transfers, purchases and logistics insert ledger evidence; they do not separately update balances. Historical money and stock are never recalculated from incomplete evidence.

`sale_payments` records received amounts, wallet, method, checkout/collection phase, reference and actor. Checkout inserts one negative customer invoice charge. Each received payment inserts one payment record, one wallet credit and one positive customer receipt, linked by payment ID. `sales.paid_amount` is the current settlement; `paid_at_checkout` preserves the initial paid amount. Checkout payment attribution is read from immutable `sale_payments`; it is not duplicated as a JSON column on `sales`. An invoice's outstanding amount is `total_amount + additional_charges - paid_amount`; customer outstanding is the negative customer-ledger sum. Unreceived amounts create no wallet entry.

Default checkout sends null/omits `p_payments` for full Cash Register payment. An explicit array supports at most 20 positive finite entries with at most two decimals; an empty array records no initial payment. A zero-total sale creates no payment. Collections target one completed sale and cannot exceed its remaining balance. Previously paid amounts without recorded methods remain paid. `get_sale_settlement.unattributed_paid` is the paid header minus attributed payment rows, and receipts show that provenance explicitly. No version flag changes settlement behavior or permits bypassing receipt validation.

Transfers append equal opposite entries with one operation ID and counterparty links. Transfers and operational settlements allow signed deficits; no available-funds gate creates a second balance policy. Adjustments append a signed entry and mandatory reason. Correct a manual balance entry by recording an opposite adjustment referencing it. Purchase payments and refunds use `purchase_payments`, immutable source-backed obligations and the same Finance request/ledger architecture. Cancellation is OWNER-only and operational; wallet refunds and transport-funding reversals are settled separately, while truck operating expenses remain intact. See the purchase lifecycle guide. Owners can cancel a sale with recorded post-opening carrying evidence and record staged partial/multi-wallet customer refunds. Original payments are never deleted; the journal and customer ledger retain compensating evidence.

## Atomicity, authorization and recovery

Checkout preserves server price validation, ordered stock locks, tenant/request serialization, stored request payload/results, and atomic rollback. Payments participate in that same database transaction. A retried key returns its original result and rejects changed payment details. The canonical migration fills missing checkout-payload keys once; saved request IDs/results remain recoverable.

Collection locks the sale before wallets, preventing competing requests from overpaying. Multi-wallet operations lock participating wallets in UUID order, consistent with purchases. `finance_requests` serializes finance request IDs and saves the original payload/result. Client session storage retains uncertain writes per tenant/user/tab and exposes recovery before another transaction. A lost HTTP response never requires a new payment key. Wallet ledger/customer payment history is append-only; explicit browser writes remain denied.

RLS uses trusted active membership and tenant-qualified foreign keys. `finance:view` or `finance:manage` permits wallet/ledger reads; `finance:manage` gates wallet editing, transfers, adjustments and collections. POS can read payment destinations through `get_payment_wallets`, which exposes names/methods without balances or account details. Existing sales/receipt permissions permit reading persisted sale payments. Purchase managers can read eligible destination names without Finance balances, and purchase view/manage permissions gate the bounded purchase settlement API. Customer permissions expose an aggregate customer balance without opening the financial ledger.

## Frontend and query boundaries

[Finance](../../src/pages/Finance.tsx), [useFinance](../../src/hooks/useFinance.ts), shared payment entries and saved-sale payment panels use TanStack Query invalidation across Finance, wallets, customers and sales. Purchase/logistics mutations also invalidate Finance. The topbar displays combined wallet funds, from real accounts.

The ledger filters on the server before sorting/pagination, using 25-row pages (RPC maximum 100), tenant/wallet/date/type/amount indexes and narrow matching rows before fetching page details. A `pg_trgm` GIN index searches an immutable document captured with each entry (description, wallet label, payment reference and customer identity). Search follows the project's space-AND/comma-OR convention; token parsing happens once, literals are quoted/escaped, and tenant/filter parameters stay bound. Very short search terms and count/amount aggregates can still scan matching history. Inclusive date filters use `Asia/Dhaka`; minimum/maximum compare absolute entry amounts. Running balances are stored per wallet and do not change when filters/sorts change. Received/paid-out totals describe matching money movements, including both sides of transfers; they are not revenue/profit reports. No full ledger is downloaded by the browser.

Customer dues are server-paged. Saved payment history is ten rows per page; receipts aggregate persisted payments by wallet snapshot/method/phase and identify later collections. Reprints show current persisted settlement and never rerun checkout. Both receipt widths use the same content formatter as previews; 58mm uses stacked product lines, 80mm uses amount-aligned product headings. USB paper width is explicit in its printer configuration because Windows queue names are not reliable width detection. Browser width is remembered per tenant/browser; choose matching media in the OS print dialog.

## Verification

Historical finance/accounting suites run at their owning migration replay boundaries. `node supabase/tests/run-unified-accounting.mjs --vps` verifies the actual current migration on a disposable PostgreSQL clone: component purchases, FIFO/cancellations, 2/1/1 exchanges, treasury, internal Logistics, four-scope reports, tenant/role isolation, concurrent operations and snapshots against raw journals. Real Auth/API and published desktop/mobile browser acceptance remain separate from these SQL tests.

Purchase payments/refunds use source-backed obligations and the canonical wallet posting path. Source records, physical stock, FIFO, wallet ledgers and journal control accounts must reconcile independently; a balanced journal alone does not establish correctness.


## Employee payments

Employee salary, named bonus and linked driver/helper payments append immutable `employee_payment_entries` and exactly one wallet debit per entry through the existing posting trigger. Salary months and revisions remain distinct from actual wallet movements. Bonuses have custom names and never change salary balances. Actual returned funds append linked payment returns and credits to the original wallets. Finance retains salary, bonus, trip payment and return classifications; removed payment categories cannot be created. Ledger details link to the employee profile under employee/payroll permission.

Linked driver/helper payments use any active real wallet with payroll and Logistics management permissions. Custom transit creation collects its full fee into Cash Register. Confirmed completion pays only remaining crew and operating costs from Cash Register, atomically with cost edits and closure. Existing payments remain canonical; completion adds no second expense recognition. Historical employee payments create no invented wallet entries. See [Employees](employees-and-payroll.md) for salary-month rules, recovery, corrections and historical evidence.

## Additional settlement costs and historical expenses

`20261026035440_dynamic_settlement_receipts.sql` reuses the original later exchange-charge mechanism: a collection header's immutable `fee` records Additional Settlement Cost, `sales.additional_charges` accumulates it, and one negative `customer_transactions.invoice_charge` creates the customer liability. Original `total_amount`, Exchange Cost (`exchange_fee`) and checkout payment snapshots remain unchanged. Recording a cost creates no business expense, wallet debit or payment. Collect payment receives it through the existing partial/multi-wallet workflow; only money actually received credits wallets.

Bottle collection requires `sales:collect_bottles`; a positive cost additionally requires `finance:manage`. Receipt, actual-product stock, customer charge and retained Finance request commit atomically. Tenant/request locks and the sale row lock serialize retries, payments and collections. Changed request payloads are rejected. Completed historical expense/fee requests replay their original result before new validation. Unsupported expense fields are rejected; they are never silently converted into customer charges. Historical `sale_bottle_expense` entries, wallet snapshots and prior customer fees remain audited and unchanged.

Physical over-receipts are saved without capping, with acknowledgement for unexpected or cumulative above-product quantities. Each sale's bottle obligation clamps at zero before aggregation, so excess bottles never cancel another sale's debt. Further collection is disabled once that sale is settled; original uncertain requests remain recoverable. Money and bottle obligations settle independently.

`get_sale_receipt` reads current identities, original transaction values, current charges/payments and all received bottles in one statement snapshot. Preview refreshes on every open and supports manual refresh; print reloads through the same builder. Receipts show Exchange Cost and Additional Settlement Cost separately, all actual received products, current total payable/paid/due and Bottle Settled independently of payment status. Payment records retain their original method/wallet snapshots.

## Configured financial entries

Finance and Dashboard use the same reviewed financial-entry form. Select a custom type, wallet, amount, date and description. The type supplies the fixed category and business percentages; an optional disclosure permits a transaction override. Revenue/capital credit cash; expenses/withdrawals debit it; equipment debits an identifiable asset instead of profit. Stock, loans, customer/supplier settlement and payroll retain their dedicated source workflows.

Settings → Financial types manages defaults, names and activation with revision guards and definition history. Wallet/source details expose actual returned-fund entry, with source locking, bounded partial returns and recovery keys. Returns retain original category/ownership even after type edits or deactivation. Cumulative cent boundaries ensure several partial returns restore exactly the original business amounts.


## Analysis and management

Finance has Analysis, Wallet Ledger, Financial Entries, Accounting Activity and Loans & Lending navigation. Accounting Activity reuses the canonical analysis breakdown for every account, with historical movements and cutoff components, token search, financial group/account/type/business/source/date/amount/direction filters and historical sorting in an Advanced filters dialog with explicit Apply/Cancel and reset. Historical cards represent posted actions, with compact account effects, a source link and one expandable set of actual journal lines. A generic Value recorded summarizes the largest absolute account movement; a selected financial group/account shows its signed change. Amount bounds apply to that displayed action value after business/type scoping. Direction selects matching account increases/decreases. These values must not be summed as cash receipts. Components remain cutoff balances by account/type/business/necessary counterparty; detailed products are reached through source/FIFO views. Adjust Balance integrates opening funds, capital investments, corrections, recorded obligations and fixed assets. Duplicate activity, spending-history, customer-dues and capital interfaces are removed; source records and accounting remain available through their canonical ledger or management operation. The [unified accounting guide](financial-analysis.md) owns the current recognition and analysis contracts. Every wallet entry contributes its classified cash and settlement legs to the action journal, settling the appropriate receivable, liability, advance or equity account. Manual costs and Interest reduce profit; owner withdrawals and principal do not. Transfers preserve consolidated cash and create no revenue or expense. Wallet corrections use equity adjustments.

Analysis aggregates the canonical journal for opening/closing positions, period movements, P&L, capital, cash flow and audit drill-downs. The business tabular table shows LPG, Stove, Logistics and additive Combined from the same report: Cash + Accounts Receivable + Supplies + Equipment = Loan + Accounts Payable + Capital + Revenue − Expenses − Withdrawals. Supplies includes inventory in custody and outstanding cylinder replacement claims; Capital includes legitimate other equity and prior retained earnings. Reporting never estimates a missing balancing amount. Future salary payments remain advances until monthly accrual; recorded obligations settle without another expense.

Borrowing, lending, repayments and asset cost/depreciation/disposal are owner-only. Money Lent uses the same loan register and recoverable operation as borrowing, with an immutable direction. Lending debits `loan_receivable` and credits the selected real wallet; collection reverses those accounts, retains the original business allocation and cannot exceed outstanding principal. Partial and full collections retain dated activity. Principal never changes revenue or expense. Monthly/product commission configuration remains removed. Owner-entered purchase rebates use a separate Company rebate journal type: debit supplier payable, credit Other Income, with no cash, price or acquisition-cost change. Existing company credit is applied without recognizing income again; cancellation restores noncash supplier credit separately from source-backed cash refunds. See the purchase module for recognition and correction rules. Interest remains a manually paid expense without automatic schedules. Cash movements retain their actual accounting date independently of recording time.

Every report uses the signed-in trusted tenant. The obsolete multi-organization selector and cross-organization grant model are removed. See the accounting guide for disposable migration/reconciliation/concurrency, intercepted browser and real Auth/published-browser acceptance commands.

Custom type categories are exactly Withdrawal, Expense, Capital, Revenue and Equipment. Internal journal accounts retain the detail needed for assets, liabilities and double-entry posting; they are not extra category choices. Financial Entries lists original custom-entry snapshots and returned funds with category, business, date and source filters. Wallet Ledger resolves payroll sources using distinct SQL variable/alias names, accepts the actual empty-string browser filters, applies AND/comma-OR token search and sorts selected business amounts consistently.


Wallet contributions use one Capital contribution operation for initial funds and later owner investments. Stock capital contributions likewise use the capital counter-account for initial and subsequent contributed inventory. Historical opening source records and journals remain original evidence; new writes do not offer a separate opening-cash or opening-stock contract. Existing opening liabilities and other opening-position records remain distinct.

Wallet transfers require two distinct active real wallets and a positive amount. One retained request ID produces exactly one debit/credit pair; changed retry payloads reject. Transfer totals count principal once. Business filters retain allocated operational movements while the displayed wallet balance remains the actual real-wallet balance.
## Staff visibility and wallet use

Finance workspace access (`finance:view`) does not grant its reports. Independent capabilities select wallet history, own/all financial entries, accounting activity, loans/lending and Product performance & FIFO. Creation, returns, transfers and wallet configuration keep separate action gates; loans, assets, capital and corrections retain their existing OWNER-only writes.

Each real wallet has independent Use, Balance and History scopes. Global capabilities supply defaults; owner-managed per-person wallet overrides replace those defaults for that wallet. Use is checked by the canonical wallet posting trigger for both transfer legs and every payment path, independently of Balance. Read RPCs return null for hidden balances and running balances, omit unauthorized histories, and never expose raw wallet tables. Payment selectors expose only usable wallet names. Individual Balance visibility works independently of Finance workspace access; the topbar shows only authorized balances and disappears when none are visible. Source-operation payment histories still show the amounts of those operations, not wallet positions.

Analysis, Accounting activity and audit-log access require Balance visibility for every wallet, including archived wallets, because aggregate Cash or audit snapshots can otherwise disclose a hidden position. Individual History access requires Finance workspace access and inherently reveals authorized movements; it does not reveal opening/current/running balances without Balance. OWNER retains all scopes. Account revision changes clear the staff domain cache when permissions or wallet scopes change; already downloaded information cannot be recalled.

## Product performance and remaining FIFO

The Finance Product performance & FIFO tab requires `finance:stock`, independently of general Analysis. The company → weight → Refill/Package table uses completed sales by business timestamp and received purchases by receipt timestamp in Asia/Dhaka. Received Gas G and Bottle B are displayed as **Refill G − B**, **Package B**, totaling G filled cylinders. Created/in-transit orders and cancelled operations are excluded. Mouth variants are aggregated only in this quantity table; the FIFO picker selects one exact product. All-time totals cover every group, not just the visible page.

The FIFO table is a current available-stock snapshot, independent of the quantity-report date range. Filled batches show linked Gas and Bottle components; empties show Bottle only. State-specific FIFO order and original shell acquisition dates/source movements remain visible. Available stock excludes dispatched custody and outstanding replacement claims; their carrying assets remain in the canonical accounting reports. Component quantities must not be added as physical cylinders.

Historical costs include their actual attributable labour and internal transport. Refill projected unit margin = current Refill price − historical Gas unit cost; future exchange gain/loss is excluded. Package projected margin uses the linked Gas + Bottle basis. Bottle contribution uses the implied Package − Refill selling-price difference, not an empty-cylinder sale price. Retail/wholesale references and effective dates are explicit; wholesale uses the same retail fallback as POS when no active wholesale price exists, labeled as a fallback. Missing prices or estimated historical costs yield unavailable margins rather than fabricated zero costs. Projections are not realized COGS or profit; rebates do not change them.
