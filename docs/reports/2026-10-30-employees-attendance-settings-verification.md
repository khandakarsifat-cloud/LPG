## Remaining boundaries

A known approved serial can be impersonated from another network; approval and HTTPS do not cryptographically prove physical device identity. This development limitation is documented in the [attendance guide](../modules/attendance.md). The old exact-source-IP gate was intentionally removed without adding a key or relay.

The actual MB20-VL continued heartbeating after deployment.
